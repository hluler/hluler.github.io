Data deletion

Local records

AisiBill has no app account or cloud record store in version 1.0. Delete individual documents, clients, items, expenses or businesses from their edit/details screens. To delete all private records, drafts, receipts, signatures, settings and caches, open Android Settings → Apps → AisiBill → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall. Export a backup first if you need to retain records. The developer cannot remotely delete or restore files on your phone.

Exports and selected providers

PDFs, images, CSVs and ZIP files saved to Downloads, another folder, email or cloud storage remain after clearing/uninstalling. Delete these copies and any shared attachments using the receiving app/provider. Revoke Contacts permission in Android Settings if desired.

Pro ownership and verification

Clearing local data removes the verification cache and does not refund or delete the Google Play purchase. Restore purchases using the purchasing Play account. For Google order history, refund eligibility or Google-held data, use Google Play and Google account controls. The dedicated verifier does not maintain a receipt database; it handles tokens transiently. Infrastructure providers may retain security/connection information under their policies.

Support correspondence

To request deletion of support emails or information voluntarily sent to AiSiFun, email leo344937116@gmail.com with subject “AisiBill data deletion”. Identify the email address used for the earlier request; do not send passwords, full card details, invoices or purchase tokens. We may need a reply from that address to verify ownership. We aim to respond within 30 days and explain any retention required by applicable law. This is an email request option, not an automatic deletion of your phone or Google account.

数据删除

本机账目

AisiBill 1.0 没有应用账号或云端账目。可在各编辑/详情页删除单据、客户、项目、支出或企业。要删除全部私有账目、草稿、票据、签名、设置及缓存，请打开 Android 设置 → 应用 → AisiBill → 存储 → 清除存储，或卸载应用。需要保留资料时请先导出备份。开发者无法远程删除或恢复手机文件。

导出文件与所选服务

保存在下载目录、其他文件夹、邮件或云端的 PDF、图片、CSV 和 ZIP 不会随清除存储或卸载删除，请通过相应应用或提供者删除副本与附件。需要时可在 Android 设置撤销通讯录权限。

高级版购买及验单

清除本机数据会移除核验缓存，不会退款或删除 Google Play 购买。可使用原购买账号恢复权益。订单记录、退款资格和 Google 持有数据请使用 Google Play 与 Google 账号管理工具。专用验单服务临时处理令牌，不保存收据数据库；基础设施提供者可能按自身政策保留安全与连接信息。

支持通信删除

如需删除主动发送给 AiSiFun 的支持邮件或资料，请发送至 leo344937116@gmail.com，主题填写“AisiBill 数据删除”，说明先前联系所用邮箱。不要发送密码、完整银行卡、发票或购买令牌。为确认所有权，可能需要原邮箱回复。我们力争在 30 天内答复，并说明适用法律要求的必要留存。此邮件入口不会自动删除手机或 Google 账号资料。
