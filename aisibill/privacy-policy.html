Privacy policy

1. Scope and offline records

This policy covers the first Android release of AisiBill. It creates and manages invoices, estimates, quotes, customers, items, business profiles, payments recorded by you, expenses and reports on your device. It has no AisiBill account, cloud synchronization, advertising, remote invoice hosting, customer card collection or automatic customer charges. Optional Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase. We do not upload invoice content, contacts, receipts, logos, signatures or backups to our purchase verifier.

2. Local information and retention

The app stores your business and customer details, document amounts and tax fields, payment notes, expense receipts, selected images, logos, signatures, drafts, preferences and recurring-draft schedules in Android private storage. Do not enter information that you are not authorized to process. Records remain until you delete them or clear app storage/uninstall. Some private media, draft snapshots and cached exports may remain after deleting an individual record. Clear storage or uninstall to remove all private app data. Android app backup is disabled. The developer cannot recover deleted local records.

3. Selected files, contacts and camera

Android document pickers grant access to files you select; chosen cloud storage providers may transfer files under their own policy. You may select one contact for import. With Contacts permission, AisiBill reads that chosen contact’s available name, phone, email and address; if permission is denied, the phone-contact picker provides a narrower fallback. It does not upload your address book. You can revoke Contacts permission in Android settings. Photos are selected through a system picker or captured by an external camera app. AisiBill does not request its own camera, microphone, location, phone-state, Bluetooth or broad media-library permission.

4. Receipt recognition and Google ML Kit

Bundled Latin and Chinese OCR recognizes selected receipt images locally; images and recognized text are not sent to Google for recognition. Review extracted values before saving an expense. Google ML Kit may contact Google for maintenance or compatibility information and sends SDK performance and usage metrics. These can include device manufacturer/model/OS, app package and version, per-installation identifiers, API configuration, input/output size, initialization/detection/release events, latency and error codes. Google uses these for diagnostics, usage analytics, maintenance, improvement and abuse detection, over HTTPS. These metrics are separate from invoice and receipt content. The application does not offer a switch to disable all ML Kit metrics. Release builds do not include the translation authoring SDK.

5. Pro purchases and verification

Google Play handles payment under your Google account and its terms. The local price comes from Google Play; AiSiFun does not receive your full payment card or banking details. After a completed purchase or restore, the app sends its Google Play purchase token over HTTPS to a dedicated AisiBill verifier operated by AiSiFun on Cloudflare Workers. The verifier checks com.aisifun.aisibill and aisibill_pro_lifetime with Google Android Publisher, validates the permanent buy option and purchase/refund state, and acknowledges a valid new purchase before granting Pro. It does not consume the product. A pending, refunded, revoked or invalid purchase grants no new Pro access.

6. Verification data and offline access

The verifier processes tokens transiently and does not keep a receipt database, invoice data or app request/receipt logs; application observability is disabled. Cloudflare and Google may process network/connection metadata such as IP addresses for providing and securing their services. The app stores verification status and a monotonic offline verification lease of up to 24 hours, scoped to this package and endpoint; tokens are not stored in app preferences or backups. Device restart or reinstall requires online restore/re-verification. Lifetime ownership is not a subscription and has no monthly expiry; the 24-hour interval is a verification limit. Network failure does not extend the cache indefinitely. Refund/revocation may be reflected only on the next successful check or lease expiry. Free tools remain usable offline.

7. Export, sharing and payment links

PDFs, images, CSVs and ZIP backups/archives are generated locally. Saving or sharing gives the selected document provider, print service, messaging app or recipient the files you choose. Exported copies and backups remain outside the app after clearing storage or uninstalling; remove them separately. Backups may contain customer data, receipt images and signatures; use password protection and store them securely. A merchant payment link that you enter opens in your browser when selected; the merchant and payment provider apply their policies. Opening a link or marking an invoice paid is not independent confirmation of a customer payment. This release does not process customer payments itself.

8. Notifications and recurring drafts

Optional Android notifications remind you of due documents. A boot receiver reschedules local reminders after a device restart. Recurring invoices are created as local drafts when the app processes due schedules; the app does not email or charge a customer automatically. Notification permission can be revoked in Android settings.

9. Support, deletion and your controls

Contact leo344937116@gmail.com for privacy, support or deletion requests. Support receives only the email address, message and attachments you voluntarily send. Avoid sending complete accounts, customer lists or unnecessary sensitive documents. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed to answer the request and meet applicable obligations; request its deletion at the same address. There is no AisiBill cloud account to delete. Use the Data deletion page for local data, exported files, Google purchases and support-email controls.

10. Security, audience and updates

Local records use Android’s app sandbox; password-protected exports use the app’s encrypted backup format. Network verification and SDK metrics use HTTPS, but no system guarantees absolute security. This is a business utility, not directed to children. Services may process data in countries where Google, Cloudflare, GitHub or the email provider operate. Opening these pages visits GitHub Pages and may disclose normal web request data to GitHub. We do not sell personal data or use advertising SDKs. Updates to this policy will appear at this URL with the applicable date and version scope.
