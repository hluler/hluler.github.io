1. Media processing and optional monetization

AisiSlim compresses, converts and edits videos and photos on your Android device. The app does not upload selected media, file names, document contents or media metadata to AiSiFun, ad partners or the purchase verifier. The application has no app account. Google Play purchases use your Google account under Google’s terms. Video and photo tools remain available without a subscription; a verified monthly subscription removes app-requested advertising. Advertising SDKs and purchase verification may process the separate data described below.

2. Files and permissions

You select videos, images and output folders with Android’s system document picker, share files to the app, or use an external camera app. AisiSlim does not request broad READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions. The system grants access only to selected documents or folders; a folder grant includes files within that folder. Persisted document permissions support queued work and reopening selected files. A provider may download a cloud document or upload an output saved to a selected cloud folder under its own policy. Non-seekable documents and temporary shares can be copied to private working storage. Photo imports and original-replacement backups are stored privately on the device. The external camera app may request its own permission; AisiSlim itself does not request camera or microphone permission. The monetized build adds INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, Google Play BILLING and AD_ID permissions for advertising/consent and secure purchase verification. It does not request precise or coarse Android location permissions, contacts, microphone or broad media permissions. IP-derived approximate location may still be processed by ad partners. Bundled SDK manifests also declare ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS and READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE for supported advertising features and basic cellular-network/software-state information. This is not READ_PHONE_STATE or READ_PHONE_NUMBERS, which are not requested. Actual collection follows the enabled SDK features and consent.

3. Metadata and exported files

Inputs may contain faces, audio, location, camera details, timestamps and other personal information. Processing reads the content and technical metadata locally. Photo metadata retention is controlled by the metadata setting; disabling it removes supported camera metadata from generated outputs. Video containers and selected streams may retain metadata. Review outputs before sharing. Generated files are saved privately and, when you choose Save or automatic photo saving is enabled, copied to your chosen document folder or the device media library. Exported copies remain after removing an in-app result or uninstalling.

4. Original replacement and restoration

Outputs are separate copies by default. Replacing an original photo requires your confirmation, compatible format and write access. The app keeps a local original backup and can restore it from the saved result. If writing is interrupted, keep the app and its backup installed and use Restore original. Do not delete working files or uninstall until you have verified your originals and saved anything you need. AiSiFun cannot restore files that you have deleted.

5. Local retention and your controls

The app stores selected file references, work copies, settings, queues, processing results, replacement backups and local diagnostic information in its private device storage. Working copies and backups may remain after removing an in-app result. In-app cleanup, where available, removes eligible unused video imports and preserves active work. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove all private working files and backups; removing exported media is a separate action. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes private files and recovery backups and does not erase externally exported copies. Android app backup is disabled. Revoke selected-document access by removing app permissions where Android or the document provider supports it, or clear app storage; you can select the files again later.

6. Background work and notifications

Foreground-service and wake-lock permissions let Android run and display ongoing local processing. Notification permission, when granted, allows progress and completion notifications. Processing still depends on Android’s background limits, available memory, storage and battery settings. The app does not request exemption from battery optimization; the optional settings link opens Android’s battery settings.

7. Sharing, external links and support

Sharing or exporting occurs when you choose Android’s share/open action, save/export, or an output folder, including automatic photo saving after you enable it. The selected receiving app, service or storage provider obtains the file and may collect, synchronize or upload it under its own policy. AisiSlim does not automatically send media to AiSiFun or other services. Opening this policy in a browser visits GitHub Pages, whose operator may process standard request information under its own privacy policy. Contacting support opens your email app. We receive the email address, message and attachments that you voluntarily send; do not send sensitive media unless necessary. Support correspondence is used to respond and is retained only as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; email deletion requests can be sent to the same address.

8. Children, transport and international access

This is a general media utility, not a service directed to children. Network traffic for advertising and verification uses the providers’ transport security and may be processed in their operating countries. Private media files use Android’s app sandbox, and external output providers remain under your control. No system guarantees absolute security. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information; contact support about an inappropriate collection. Store audience declarations and ad-partner age settings must match the actual audience before public distribution.

9. Ads, consent and partner data

The binary bundles Unity LevelPlay (including ironSource Ads and Ad Quality), Unity Ads, Google UMP and the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK through the AdMob adapter. One LevelPlay flow controls app-requested ads. AdMob serving is disabled in this integration and reserved for later configuration. SDK startup components may prepare storage or process technical information before an ad object is created; disabling app ad requests is not a promise that every SDK performs zero processing. Depending on enabled partners, consent and features, providers may process IP-derived approximate location, advertising/app-set/device identifiers, ad interactions, app/SDK performance and diagnostics; Unity Ads also discloses purchase-history data in its SDK guidance. Purposes include ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Media files are not supplied to the ad SDKs.
The app requests current UMP consent information and shows required forms before initializing app-requested ads. A prior valid consent state may be reused only when UMP permits requests. Where required, Advertising privacy choices is available in Settings/the subscription page; changing choices invalidates preloaded app ad objects. An active verified subscription stops new ad initialization, load and display requests by the app. The subscription does not disable Google Play, the verification service or every SDK startup component. Reset/delete the Android ad ID in device settings and use providers’ privacy controls for their data.
