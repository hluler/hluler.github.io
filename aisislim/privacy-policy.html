1. Local media processing
AisiSlim compresses photos and processes videos on your Android device. Selected media, file names, file contents and media metadata are not uploaded to AiSiFun or the purchase verifier. This version contains no advertising, analytics or consent-management SDKs and does not request the Android advertising ID. The app has no app account. Photo compression is free; video tools and advanced photo editing require a one-time Premium purchase. There is no automatic renewal.

2. Selected files and Android permissions
The app uses Android's system document picker for selected photos, videos and output folders, accepts files you explicitly share, and can open an external camera app. It does not request broad READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, camera or microphone permissions. A selected folder grant can cover files within that folder. Persisted grants support reopening selected files and queued work. The selected provider may download a cloud document or synchronize saved outputs under its own policy. Working copies, drafts, thumbnails, history and photo replacement backups are stored in private app storage. Exports remain in the destination you choose. A foreground processing notification, wake lock and optional notification permission support visible long-running work; no battery-optimization exemption is requested.

3. Purchases and verification
Google Play handles payment using your Google account. AiSiFun does not receive your full payment-card or banking details. Google may process purchase and device information under https://policies.google.com/privacy. To deliver and restore Premium, the app sends only the Play purchase token over HTTPS to https://aisislim-billing.david-he1986.workers.dev/verify-premium. Our verifier uses a dedicated service identity limited to this app to check the fixed package, product, purchase option, purchase status, refund/consumption status and acknowledgment with Google Play. A pending or invalid payment does not unlock Premium. Tokens are processed transiently; the verifier has no receipt database, application request logging or application observability enabled. It does not receive your media, file names or Google account email. Google and Cloudflare infrastructure may process connection information, including IP addresses, for delivery, security and operation under their policies. Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

4. Local verification cache
The app stores the product ID, verified entitlement and a monotonic verification cache for at most 24 hours. This cache is not a subscription or an expiry of the one-time purchase. Device restart or cache expiry requires online re-verification. A failed connection does not create a new entitlement. Refund/revocation may not immediately invalidate an existing offline cache. Restore purchases checks the same Google Play account and the verifier. Purchase terms: https://hluler.github.io/aisislim/purchase-terms.html.

5. Local control, deletion and exports
You can remove imports, drafts and results through app controls. Removing a local result does not delete an exported copy. Replacing an original uses Android's granted document access and keeps a private backup until you restore or remove it; check your outputs before removing backups. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes private working data and settings, including private backups, but not exported files or Google Play purchase ownership. Use Android settings to revoke persisted document permissions. Retain originals and exports you need.

6. Sharing, websites and support
Only an action you choose, such as Share, Open, Export or automatic saving you enable, supplies an output to your selected app or storage provider. That recipient may synchronize or upload it under its own policy. Opening this policy visits GitHub Pages, whose operator may process normal request information. Support opens your email app. AiSiFun receives only the email, message and attachments you choose to send, uses them to answer your request, and retains correspondence only as reasonably needed to resolve it or meet applicable obligations. Request deletion at leo344937116@gmail.com. Do not send sensitive media unnecessarily.

7. Audience, security and rights
The app is an adult-oriented media utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Network purchase verification uses HTTPS; local private data uses Android's app sandbox. No security mechanism guarantees absolute security. Service providers may process data in countries outside yours. Contact leo344937116@gmail.com for access, correction, deletion or privacy questions regarding data you provide to us; Google or your chosen recipient controls its own records and rights procedures. Changes are published at the policy URL with an updated effective date.

8. Earlier versions
Internal 1.1.0 builds bundled advertising/CMP SDKs and an optional monthly ad-removal subscription. These are removed from 1.2.0; no new monthly plan is offered in this app. Older installed versions may continue their earlier behavior until updated. Existing subscription orders remain managed by Google Play; uninstalling does not cancel them. Historical terms remain at https://hluler.github.io/aisislim/subscription-terms.html. Contact support if an earlier paid order needs assistance.
