AisiSlim Privacy Policy

App: AisiSlim (com.aisifun.aisislim)

1. On-device processing; no account

AisiSlim compresses, converts and edits videos and photos on your Android device. This release has no accounts, advertising SDKs, subscriptions, analytics, crash-upload service or app-operated server. The app has no Android INTERNET permission. AiSiFun does not receive your media, file names, metadata, processing history, device identifiers or usage activity through this app.

2. Files and permissions

You select videos, images and output folders with Android’s system document picker, share files to the app, or use an external camera app. AisiSlim does not request broad READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions. The system grants access only to selected documents or folders; a folder grant includes files within that folder. Persisted document permissions support queued work and reopening selected files. A provider may download a cloud document or upload an output saved to a selected cloud folder under its own policy. Non-seekable documents and temporary shares can be copied to private working storage. Photo imports and original-replacement backups are stored privately on the device. The external camera app may request its own permission; AisiSlim itself does not request camera or microphone permission.

3. Metadata and exported files

Inputs may contain faces, audio, location, camera details, timestamps and other personal information. Processing reads the content and technical metadata locally. Photo metadata retention is controlled by the metadata setting; disabling it removes supported camera metadata from generated outputs. Video containers and selected streams may retain metadata. Review outputs before sharing. Generated files are saved privately and, when you choose Save or automatic photo saving is enabled, copied to your chosen document folder or the device media library. Exported copies remain after removing an in-app result or uninstalling.

4. Original replacement and restoration

Outputs are separate copies by default. Replacing an original photo requires your confirmation, compatible format and write access. The app keeps a local original backup and can restore it from the saved result. If writing is interrupted, keep the app and its backup installed and use Restore original. Do not delete working files or uninstall until you have verified your originals and saved anything you need. AiSiFun cannot restore files that you have deleted.

5. Local retention and your controls

The app stores selected file references, work copies, settings, queues, processing results, replacement backups and local diagnostic information in its private device storage. Working copies and backups may remain after removing an in-app result. In-app cleanup, where available, removes eligible unused video imports and preserves active work. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall to remove all private working files and backups; removing exported media is a separate action. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes private files and recovery backups and does not erase externally exported copies. Android app backup is disabled. Revoke selected-document access by removing app permissions where Android or the document provider supports it, or clear app storage; you can select the files again later.

6. Background work and notifications

Foreground-service and wake-lock permissions let Android run and display ongoing local processing. Notification permission, when granted, allows progress and completion notifications. Processing still depends on Android’s background limits, available memory, storage and battery settings. The app does not request exemption from battery optimization; the optional settings link opens Android’s battery settings.

7. Sharing, external links and support

Sharing or exporting occurs when you choose Android’s share/open action, save/export, or an output folder, including automatic photo saving after you enable it. The selected receiving app, service or storage provider obtains the file and may collect, synchronize or upload it under its own policy. AisiSlim does not automatically send media to AiSiFun or other services. Opening this policy in a browser visits GitHub Pages, whose operator may process standard request information under its own privacy policy. Contacting support opens your email app. We receive the email address, message and attachments that you voluntarily send; do not send sensitive media unless necessary. Support correspondence is used to respond and is retained only as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; email deletion requests can be sent to the same address.

8. Children, security and international access

The app is a general media utility and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. Private files use Android’s app sandbox. Device security, external storage providers and receiving apps remain under your control; no storage mechanism guarantees absolute security. Since there is no app-operated server or automatic collection, AisiSlim does not transfer app data internationally. Email support and voluntarily chosen external services may process data in their operating regions.

9. Google Play Data Safety

For this release, the app collects no user data off the device and shares no user data automatically. Local processing is not off-device collection. User-directed exports, external document providers, browser visits and support email are explained above. Google Play declarations must match this release; future SDKs or network features require a fresh review and an updated policy before release.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when processing practices change. If you have questions or wish to request deletion of support correspondence, contact AiSiFun at leo344937116@gmail.com. All local advanced tools in this release are included without advertising, subscriptions or feature purchases.

AisiSlim（轻压）隐私政策

1. 本机处理，无需账号

AisiSlim 在 Android 设备本机压缩、转换和编辑视频与图片。本版本没有账号、广告 SDK、订阅、使用情况分析、崩溃上传服务或开发者运营的服务器，未声明 Android INTERNET 网络权限。AiSiFun 不会通过本应用接收您的媒体、文件名、元数据、处理历史、设备标识符或使用活动。

2. 文件和权限

您通过 Android 系统文件选择器选取视频、图片或输出文件夹，也可以将文件分享到本应用，或调用外部相机应用。本应用不申请 READ_MEDIA_IMAGES、READ_MEDIA_VIDEO 或 READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE 广泛媒体读取权限。系统仅授予您所选文档或文件夹的访问权；文件夹授权包含其内部文件。持久文档授权支持排队处理和重新打开已选择文件。文档提供者可能按其自身政策下载云文档，或上传您保存到所选云文件夹的输出文件。无法直接随机读取的文档及临时分享可能被复制到应用私有工作目录，图片导入副本和原图替换备份也保存在设备私有目录。外部相机应用可能自行申请权限；AisiSlim 本身不申请相机或麦克风权限。

3. 元数据与导出副本

输入媒体可能含有人像、声音、位置、相机信息、时间戳和其他个人信息。内容及技术元数据仅在本机读取和处理。图片的元数据设置决定是否保留受支持的相机元数据；关闭后会在生成结果中移除这些元数据。视频容器和保留的轨道仍可能包含元数据，分享前请检查结果。生成文件首先保存在私有目录；您选择保存或开启图片自动保存后，结果会复制到选定文件夹或设备媒体库。移除应用内结果或卸载应用不会删除已导出的副本。

4. 替换与恢复原图

默认生成独立副本。替换原图需要您的确认、兼容格式和写入权限。应用保留本机原始备份，并可在已保存的结果中恢复原图。若写入中断，请保留应用和备份，使用“恢复原图”。核对原图并导出所需文件前，不要删除工作文件或卸载应用。AiSiFun 无法恢复您已删除的文件。

5. 本机留存与管理

应用在设备私有目录保存已选文件引用、工作副本、设置、队列、处理结果、替换备份和本机诊断信息。移除应用内结果后，工作副本和备份仍可能保留。可用的应用内清理会删除符合条件的未使用视频导入副本并保留活动任务。要删除所有私有工作文件和备份，请在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载应用；删除已导出媒体属于单独操作。清除存储或卸载将删除私有文件及恢复备份，但不会删除外部导出副本。应用关闭了 Android 应用备份。在 Android 或文档提供者支持的设置中撤销所选文档授权，或清除应用存储后，可重新选择需要的文件。

6. 后台处理与通知

前台服务和唤醒锁权限用于 Android 本机处理及显示进度。允许通知权限后，可显示进度和完成通知。处理仍受系统后台限制、内存、存储和电池设置影响。应用不申请忽略电池优化的豁免权限；可选设置入口仅打开 Android 电池设置。

7. 分享、外部链接与支持

您选择 Android 分享或打开、保存或导出、或选定输出文件夹时，会向所选接收方提供文件；启用图片自动保存后，也会写入该文件夹。所选应用、服务或存储提供者可能根据其自身政策收集、同步或上传文件。AisiSlim 不会自动将媒体发送给 AiSiFun 或其他服务。用浏览器查看本政策会访问 GitHub Pages，其运营方可能按自身政策处理常规请求信息。联系支持会打开您的邮件应用；我们会收到您主动发送的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，请勿发送不必要的敏感媒体。支持邮件仅用于处理您的请求，并在解决问题和满足适用义务所需的合理期限内保留；可通过同一邮箱申请删除支持通信。

8. 儿童、安全及跨境

本应用是通用媒体工具，不会通过应用主动收集儿童个人信息。私有文件受到 Android 应用沙盒保护；设备安全、外部存储提供者和接收应用由您选择和管理，没有任何存储方式能保证绝对安全。应用没有开发者服务器或自动数据收集，不会自动跨境传输应用数据。支持邮件和您主动选择的外部服务可能在其运营地区处理信息。

9. Google Play 数据安全声明

本版本不向设备外收集用户数据，也不自动共享用户数据。本机处理不属于离机收集。用户主动导出、外部文档提供者、浏览器访问和支持邮件的处理如上所述。Google Play 表单必须与此版本的实际实现一致；未来加入 SDK 或联网功能前，需要重新评估并更新政策。

10. 政策变更与联系

处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页面及生效日期。问题或支持通信删除请求请联系 AiSiFun：leo344937116@gmail.com。本版本全部本机高级工具均已开放，无广告、订阅或功能内购。
