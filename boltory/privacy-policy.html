Privacy Policy — BOLTORY

App: BOLTORY (螺趣工厂) · Developer: AiSiFun
Package: com.aisifun.screwworkshop
Updated: 23 October 2026

BOLTORY is a screw-removal puzzle game. You can play its levels offline without registering an app account. The app includes Google AdMob advertising and Google Play Games features. This policy explains what stays on your device, what those services handle, and how to manage your choices.

1. Game progress and support

Level progress, the current puzzle, coins, tools and settings are saved locally. We do not operate a server that receives these local saves and do not provide our own cloud-save service. Android backup or device migration may copy app data according to your device and account settings.

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to address your request and retain it only as necessary for that purpose and applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive details.

2. Advertising

Google AdMob may show a banner on the home screen, occasional interstitial ads after completed levels, and optional rewarded ads for extra tools or revival time. Banner ads are hidden during puzzles and dialogs. A rewarded-ad button describes the benefit before you choose to continue. Offline gameplay remains available when advertising cannot load.

Google's advertising SDK processes and may share IP-derived approximate location, device and account identifiers (including advertising or app-set IDs), app/ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These support ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK traffic uses TLS encryption. We can see aggregated ad performance in AdMob. No Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics SDK is included.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's use of information from partner apps for its processing, partners and international transfers.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates consent information at launch and displays a message when required. Production ads are requested only when UMP allows requests. If there is no usable consent status, the puzzle remains playable without those requests. Where required, Settings → Ad privacy options / 设置 → 广告隐私选项 lets you revisit your choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through supported Android settings. Declining personalised ads or resetting an ID does not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing or erase previously collected data.

4. Google Play Games

Play Games may authenticate using your Google Play Games profile, subject to your Google settings. The app uses the service for sign-in, achievements and a leaderboard. When authenticated, eligible completed levels are submitted to Google for score and achievement updates. Profile presentation and leaderboard visibility follow Google's controls. Google may process player/account identifiers, gameplay activity, device information and diagnostics to operate these features. We do not receive your Google password, request email or contacts access, or maintain a separate BOLTORY account database.

These features require connectivity and a configured, available Google service. They do not upload the full local puzzle save, and a leaderboard score is not a cloud-save backup. You can continue offline when authentication is unavailable.

5. Permissions and security

The app uses network access, network-state information, advertising-related permissions and optional vibration. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, storage-library or precise-location permission. Google services use encrypted connections. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention, deletion and requests

To remove local game progress, clear BOLTORY's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Also review Android backup settings if you want to remove backup copies. These actions do not delete data already held by Google or its advertising partners.

Manage Play Games data using Google's delete game data or Play Games profile instructions. Removing Google-held game data and removing local saves are separate actions. Google determines retention of data it controls under its policies. For information you sent directly to us, contact the address below to request access, correction or deletion, or to exercise applicable privacy rights.

7. Intended audience

BOLTORY is intended for people aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request and delete information we control where appropriate. For data held by Google, use its privacy and deletion controls described above.

8. Updates and contact

Changes will appear on this page with an updated date. Privacy and support contact: leo344937116@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 螺趣工厂

应用：螺趣工厂 / BOLTORY · 开发者：AiSiFun
包名：com.aisifun.screwworkshop
更新日期：2026年10月23日

螺趣工厂是一款拆卸螺丝的益智游戏，无需注册本应用账号即可离线游玩。应用包含Google AdMob广告及Google Play Games功能。本政策说明本地数据、第三方服务处理的信息，以及您的管理方式。

1. 游戏进度与用户支持

关卡进度、当前棋盘、金币、工具和设置保存在本机。我们不运营接收这些存档的服务器，也不提供自建云存档服务。Android备份或设备迁移可能依照您的系统和账号设置复制应用数据。

如果您发邮件联系我们，我们会收到您的邮箱及主动提供的信息，用于回应请求，并仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留。请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感信息。

2. 广告

Google AdMob可能在首页展示横幅广告，在部分已完成关卡的切换处展示插屏，并提供自愿观看的激励广告以补充工具或增加复活时间。横幅在棋盘操作和弹窗期间隐藏；激励按钮会事先说明奖励。广告加载失败时仍可离线游玩。

Google广告SDK会处理并可能共享由IP推断的大致位置、设备和账号标识符（包括广告ID或应用集ID）、应用和广告互动、诊断或性能信息，用于广告提供及衡量、分析、防止欺诈或无效流量。SDK使用TLS加密传输。我们可以在AdMob查看汇总广告效果；本应用没有加入Firebase Analytics或Crashlytics SDK。

第三方处理、合作伙伴及跨境传输详情，请参阅Google隐私政策及Google如何使用合作应用的信息。

3. 同意与广告选择

Google用户消息平台（UMP）会在启动时更新同意状态，并按要求展示消息。正式版只有在UMP允许时才请求广告；没有可用同意状态时仍可继续玩游戏。在需要提供入口的地区，您可以通过设置 → 广告隐私选项重新管理选择。

您也可以在支持的Android设置中重置或删除广告ID。拒绝个性化广告或重置ID并不必然停止所有广告相关处理，也不会自动删除此前收集的信息。

4. Google Play Games

Play Games可能根据您的Google设置使用游戏资料进行身份验证。本应用使用登录、成就和排行榜功能；认证后会向Google提交符合规则的通关成绩及成就更新。游戏资料展示和排行榜可见性由Google相关控制决定。Google可能处理玩家或账号标识、游戏活动、设备信息和诊断信息，以提供这些功能。我们不会取得您的Google密码，不申请读取邮箱或通讯录，也不维护独立的BOLTORY账号数据库。

这些功能需要联网及已配置且可用的Google服务。完整的本地棋盘存档不会因此上传；排行榜成绩不能用来恢复完整存档。认证不可用时仍可离线游戏。

5. 权限与安全

应用使用网络、网络状态、广告相关权限及可选振动，不请求相机、麦克风、通讯录、媒体库或精确位置权限。Google服务使用加密连接；任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。

6. 保留、删除与请求

通过Android设置清除本应用存储或卸载应用，可移除本地进度；如需移除备份，还请检查Android备份设置。这些操作不会删除Google或广告合作伙伴已持有的数据。

Play Games数据可按Google的删除游戏数据或Play Games资料说明管理。删除Google端数据与删除本地存档是不同操作；Google自行控制的数据按其政策保留。对于您直接提供给我们的信息，可通过下方邮箱申请访问、更正、删除或行使适用隐私权利。

7. 适用人群

螺趣工厂面向13岁及以上人群，不面向13岁以下儿童。我们不会明知而主动收集13岁以下儿童的个人信息。如您认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们核查，并在适当情况下删除由我们控制的信息。Google持有的数据，请通过上文列出的Google隐私及删除工具管理。

8. 更新与联系
