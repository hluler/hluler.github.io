Privacy Policy — Xianting Cards

App: Xianting Cards (闲庭棋牌)
Package: com.aisifun.doudizhu
Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy describes information handled by Xianting Cards and its integrated services. The app offers offline Dou Dizhu, Guandan and Tractor (80 Points) games. No account or login is required. We do not operate a game server to receive your cards, game saves or scores.

1. Local game data and support

Current games, settings, game records and ad-free deal benefits are stored on your device. The app does not provide a cloud-save or account-sync service. Backup is disabled in the app configuration; device transfer behaviour may also depend on Android and the device manufacturer.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, and use it to respond. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to display occasional interstitial ads at completed-deal transitions. Optional rewarded interstitial ads offer the next three deals without ordinary interstitial ads. A reward description and a skip option appear before a rewarded ad. The underlying card games and ordinary hint features remain available without watching rewarded ads.

Google's advertising services may collect and share:

  • IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them;
  • advertising or device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, including ad views and taps;
  • device information, diagnostics and app or SDK performance information.

Google and its advertising partners use this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics, and prevent fraud or invalid traffic. Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. We may view aggregated advertising performance reports in AdMob.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services for its data practices, including international processing.

3. Consent and privacy choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information at app launch and present privacy messages when required, including in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Production ad requests are enabled only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. If consent cannot be obtained and there is no valid prior consent status, offline gameplay continues without requesting ads.

Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, you can revisit your choices in Settings → Ad privacy options (设置 → 广告隐私选项). You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings where supported. Declining personalised ads or resetting an ID does not necessarily stop all advertising-related processing. Rewarded ads can be skipped.

4. Permissions

The app and advertising SDK use internet access, network-state information, advertising-related permissions and services, and optional game vibration. The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise location access.

5. Retention, deletion and requests

Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to delete local game data and ad-free benefits. These actions do not delete data already held independently by Google or its partners; their retention and deletion practices are described in the linked policies. Resetting an advertising ID does not itself erase information previously collected.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, or to exercise applicable privacy rights. We cannot directly delete data controlled independently by Google.

6. Intended audience

Xianting Cards is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

7. Changes and contact

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at leo344937116@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 闲庭棋牌

应用：闲庭棋牌 / Xianting Cards
包名：com.aisifun.doudizhu
更新日期：2026年10月19日

本政策说明闲庭棋牌及其集成服务如何处理信息。应用提供单机斗地主、掼蛋和80分，不要求注册或登录。我们不运营接收您的手牌、牌局存档或分数的游戏服务器。

1. 本地游戏数据与用户支持

当前牌局、设置、战绩和免插屏福利保存在本机。应用不提供云存档或账号同步服务；应用配置已关闭备份，设备迁移行为还可能取决于Android系统和设备厂商。

如果您通过邮件联系开发者，我们会收到您的邮箱地址及主动提供的信息，并用于回应请求。请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感信息。

2. 广告与第三方服务

应用使用Google AdMob，在部分已完成牌局的切换处展示普通插屏广告，并提供可选择的激励插屏广告，奖励为接下来3副免普通插屏。展示激励广告前会说明奖励并提供跳过选项。单机游戏及原有普通提示功能无需观看激励广告即可使用。

Google广告服务可能收集和共享：

  • IP地址及由此推断的大致位置；
  • 广告或设备标识符，例如Android广告ID和应用集ID；
  • 应用与广告互动，例如广告展示和点击；
  • 设备信息、诊断及应用或SDK性能信息。

Google及其广告合作伙伴使用这些信息提供和衡量广告、进行分析、防止欺诈或无效流量。Google移动广告SDK的数据在传输中加密。我们可在AdMob查看汇总广告效果报告。

有关数据处理及跨境处理，请参阅Google隐私政策和Google如何使用合作应用的信息。

3. 同意与隐私选择

应用使用Google用户消息平台（UMP）在启动时更新隐私同意信息，并按要求展示隐私消息，包括适用于欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士的消息。正式版本仅在UMP允许请求广告后加载广告；如果无法取得同意且没有有效的既有状态，仍可继续单机游戏，不请求广告。

在Google要求提供隐私选项入口的地区，可在设置 → 广告隐私选项重新管理选择。您也可以在支持的Android系统设置中重置或删除广告ID。拒绝个性化广告或重置标识符，不必然停止所有广告相关数据处理。激励广告可跳过。

4. 权限

应用及广告SDK使用网络访问、网络状态、广告相关权限或系统服务，以及可选的游戏振动。应用不要求访问通讯录、相机、麦克风或设备精确位置。

5. 保留、删除与请求

通过Android设置清除应用存储或卸载应用可删除本地游戏数据和免插屏福利。这不会删除Google及其合作伙伴已独立持有的信息，相关保留和删除规则以上述第三方政策为准；重置广告ID本身也不会删除此前收集的信息。

我们仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留支持邮件。您可联系开发者申请访问、更正或删除我们持有的信息，或行使适用的隐私权利。我们不能直接删除由Google独立控制的数据。

6. 适用人群

闲庭棋牌面向18岁及以上成年人，不针对儿童。如果您认为儿童曾向我们提供个人信息，请联系我们调查并采取适当措施。

7. 更新与联系

政策变更会在本页发布并更新日期。如有隐私问题或信息请求，请联系开发者：leo344937116@gmail.com。
