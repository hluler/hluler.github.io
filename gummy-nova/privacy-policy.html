Privacy Policy — Gummy Nova

Developer: AiSiFun / ljstu
Android package: com.aisifun.gummynova
Applies to version 1.0.0 (12), updated 3 November 2026

Gummy Nova is an offline bubble-shooter game. This first public edition has no advertising, in-app purchases, account registration, Google Play Games sign-in, online leaderboards, cloud saves, or third-party analytics.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores your current board and unfinished shot, scores, completed levels, stars, virtual coins, booster inventory, language and gameplay settings in the application's private storage on your device. These records are used to resume play and remember preferences. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app disables Android application backup and does not provide its own cloud synchronization or account system.

2. Collection, sharing and third parties

The game does not collect or share personal data, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, recordings, game saves or usage analytics. The release does not include Google Mobile Ads, UMP, Play Games, Unity/ironSource, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics SDKs. It has no internet or network-state permission and makes no in-app network requests.

Google Play and Android may process store, installation or operating-system information under their own policies. That processing is separate from data collected by this game.

3. Support communications

If you voluntarily email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your request and keep it only as needed for the request and applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords, government identifiers or unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell game saves or support messages.

4. Age range

The app is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. It does not ask for a name, birth date or age profile. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address the request and delete information where appropriate.

5. Permissions, retention and deletion

The app may use the normal vibration permission for device feedback. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, storage-library or location access. Local progress remains until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. These actions permanently remove progress from the device; there is no cloud copy to restore. No game account needs to be deleted.

For information sent directly to support, you may request access, correction or deletion using the email below. We ask only for information necessary to identify and respond to the request. Device privacy and security also depend on your Android settings; no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes and contact

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. If a future version adds online features or advertising, this policy and the store's disclosures will be updated for that version.

隐私政策 — Gummy Nova

开发者：AiSiFun / ljstu
Android 包名：com.aisifun.gummynova
适用于 1.0.0（12）版，更新日期：2026年11月3日

Gummy Nova 是一款离线泡泡射击消除游戏。首个公开版本没有广告、内购、账号注册、Google Play Games 登录、在线排行榜、云存档或第三方统计。

1. 保存在本机的数据

当前棋盘、尚未完成的射击、分数、已完成关卡、星级、虚拟金币、道具数量、语言和游戏设置保存在本设备的应用私有存储中，用于继续游戏和记住偏好。这些记录不会发送到开发者运营的服务器。应用关闭了 Android 应用备份，不提供自建云同步或账号系统。

2. 数据收集、分享与第三方

本游戏不收集或分享个人数据、广告标识符、位置、通讯录、照片、录音、游戏存档或使用统计。正式包没有集成 Google Mobile Ads、UMP、Play Games、Unity/ironSource、Firebase Analytics 或 Crashlytics SDK，没有互联网或网络状态权限，也不在应用内发起网络请求。

Google Play 和 Android 可能根据其自身政策处理商店、安装或操作系统信息，这与本游戏自行收集数据属于不同事项。

3. 用户支持

如果您主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱和邮件中提供的信息，仅用于回应请求，并在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留。请勿发送密码、政府证件或不必要的敏感信息。我们不出售游戏存档或支持邮件。

4. 年龄范围

本应用面向13岁及以上玩家，不以13岁以下儿童为目标受众，不索取姓名、出生日期或年龄档案。家长或监护人如认为儿童向支持邮箱提供了个人信息，可联系我们处理请求，并在适用时删除相关信息。

5. 权限、保留与删除

应用可能使用普通振动权限提供设备反馈，不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录、媒体库或位置权限。本地进度会保留到您在 Android 设置中清除应用存储或卸载应用；这些操作会永久删除设备上的进度，没有可恢复的云端副本。您无需删除游戏账号，因为本游戏没有账号系统。

对于直接提供给支持的信息，您可通过下方邮箱申请访问、更正或删除。我们仅会要求识别和回应请求所必需的信息。设备隐私与安全也取决于您的 Android 设置，任何系统均无法保证绝对安全。

政策变化会在本页公布并更新日期。未来版本如增加联网功能或广告，会同步更新对应政策和商店披露。
