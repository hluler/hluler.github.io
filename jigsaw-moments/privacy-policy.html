Privacy Policy — Jigsaw Moments

App: Jigsaw Moments (拼图时光)
Developer: AiSiFun
Package: com.aisifun.jigsawworkshop
Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy describes information handled by Jigsaw Moments and its service providers. Playing puzzles does not require creating an account with us. We do not operate an account or cloud-save service for your puzzle saves or personal photos.

1. Local puzzle data and support

The app stores puzzle layouts and progress, completed-puzzle records, elapsed play time, daily-puzzle history, preferences, imported photos and downloaded artwork on your device. These data let you resume puzzles and play built-in or downloaded pictures offline. Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device and system settings.

When you email support, we receive your email address and any details or attachments you provide. We use this information to answer your request. Please include only what is needed and avoid sending passwords or sensitive photos.

2. Online artwork and website hosting

The app requests public artwork catalogs, thumbnails and full-size pictures over HTTPS from jigsaw.liteps.cc, hosted by Cloudflare. These requests expose ordinary connection information, including your IP address, requested resource, request time and technical headers, to the hosting provider. Cloudflare may process it to deliver content, maintain security and diagnose service issues.

Artwork requests do not include your imported photos, unfinished puzzle saves, completion history or Google player ID. Downloaded pictures are cached on the device for later use. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; loading it sends web requests to GitHub. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Advertising

Android versions integrate Google AdMob for occasional interstitial ads after completed puzzles and optional rewarded interstitial ads when requesting a hint. The browser version does not use the native advertising SDK. Clearly labelled offline ad demonstrations in test builds are local simulations; Google test-ad requests still contact Google's services.

Depending on the SDK and applicable settings, advertising services collect and share information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location estimated from it;
  • advertising, device and applicable account identifiers, such as Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and advertisement interactions, including app launches, ad views and taps;
  • device details and diagnostic information about app or SDK performance.

Google and advertising partners use these data to deliver and measure ads, analyse performance and prevent invalid traffic or fraud. Google Mobile Ads SDK transmissions use TLS encryption. We may access advertising performance reports in AdMob. Provider practices, including international processing, are described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of information from apps using its services.

4. Google Play Games

Android versions support Google Play Games for achievements and a leaderboard based on the total number of pieces in completed public puzzles. Google manages account authentication and the player profile according to your Google and Play Games settings. When authenticated, the app can read your player ID and display name and send achievement-unlock events and the cumulative score to Google.

The app keeps a local record of credited public puzzles for each player to avoid counting the same completion repeatedly. It excludes puzzles made from personal photos from this calculation. We do not send imported photos, photo names or unfinished puzzle layouts to Play Games or request access to your email, contacts or Google Drive. This feature does not provide cloud saves. Google controls the visibility of your profile, achievements and scores through its service settings. Puzzle play remains available when Play Games is unavailable.

5. Consent and controls

Production advertising uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check consent status and display a privacy message when required, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Commercial ads are requested only when UMP allows them. When a privacy-options entry is required, use Settings → Ad privacy choices. Test builds may request Google test ads when the consent service is unavailable.

Supported Android advertising settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID; menu names differ between devices. Changing the ID or declining personalised ads does not necessarily end all advertising-related processing. Rewarded ads are optional. You can manage Play Games profile visibility and game data in Google's settings, and system backups in your device or backup provider's settings.

6. Photos and permissions

Choosing to make a puzzle from a photo opens the system file or photo picker. The app reads the image you select, prepares a local copy for the puzzle and saves it on your device. It does not upload the selected image to our artwork server, advertising services or Play Games. A cloud-connected picker or Android backup may independently handle copies according to your settings and that provider's policy.

The app and integrated SDKs use network access, network-state information and advertising-related permissions or system services. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location, or broad access to your photo library.

7. Retention, deletion and requests

You can delete imported photos and puzzle records in My puzzles. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local data, including downloaded pictures and local Play Games sync records. Manage backups separately; restoring a backup may restore earlier data. Removing a local puzzle record does not retract achievements or scores already sent to Google.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle requests and fulfil applicable obligations. Google, Cloudflare and GitHub retain independently processed information under their own policies and settings. Clearing local data or resetting an advertising ID does not erase information already held by these providers.

Use the contact below for access, correction, deletion or other requests concerning information we hold. For a Play Games profile or other information held independently by a provider, use that provider's privacy and account-management channels. We cannot directly erase their independently held records.

8. Children and guardians

The app does not ask for a date of birth. Parents or guardians can manage device advertising, app-data, backup and Google account controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. The providers' policies linked above describe their processing.

9. Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the AiSiFun developer of Jigsaw Moments at leo344937116@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 拼图时光

应用：Jigsaw Moments（拼图时光）
开发者：AiSiFun
包名：com.aisifun.jigsawworkshop
更新日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日

本政策说明 Jigsaw Moments 及其服务提供商如何处理信息。拼图无需向我们注册账号，我们不提供用于保存拼图存档或个人照片的自建账号及云存档服务。

1. 本地拼图数据与用户支持

应用在设备本地保存拼块布局与进度、完成记录、游戏用时、每日拼图记录、偏好设置、导入照片和下载图片，供你续玩以及离线使用内置或已下载图片。Android 可能根据设备和系统设置备份或迁移应用数据。

你通过邮件联系支持时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址以及你提供的说明或附件，用于处理请求。请仅提供必要内容，避免发送密码或敏感照片。

2. 在线图库与网页托管

应用通过 HTTPS 向由 Cloudflare 托管的 jigsaw.liteps.cc 请求公开图库目录、缩略图和完整图片。托管服务商会接触普通连接信息，包括 IP 地址、请求资源、时间和技术请求头，并可能用于内容分发、安全及服务诊断。

图库请求不附带导入照片、未完成拼图存档、完成历史或 Google 玩家 ID。下载图片缓存在本机，以便后续使用。本隐私网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，加载页面会向 GitHub 发送网页请求。相关处理方式见 Cloudflare 隐私政策和 GitHub 隐私声明。

3. 广告

Android 版本集成 Google AdMob，在部分拼图完成后展示插页式广告，并在请求提示时提供可选的插页式激励广告。浏览器版本不使用原生广告 SDK。测试包中明确标注的离线广告演示仅在本地模拟；Google 测试广告请求仍会连接 Google 服务。

根据 SDK 和适用设置，广告服务会收集并分享以下类型的信息：

  • IP 地址及由其估计的大致位置；
  • 广告、设备和适用的账号标识符，例如 Android 广告 ID 和应用集 ID；
  • 应用与广告互动，例如应用启动、广告观看和点击；
  • 设备信息及应用或 SDK 性能相关的诊断信息。

Google 和广告合作伙伴将这些信息用于提供与衡量广告、分析性能及防范无效流量或欺诈。Google 移动广告 SDK 使用 TLS 加密传输，我们可在 AdMob 后台查看广告效果报表。有关服务商的处理方式（包括跨境处理），请参阅 Google 隐私政策及 Google 对使用其服务的应用信息的说明。

4. Google Play Games

Android 版本支持 Google Play Games，用于成就及按已完成公共拼图片数累计的排行榜。Google 根据你的 Google 和 Play Games 设置管理账号认证及玩家资料。已认证时，应用可读取玩家 ID 和显示名称，并向 Google 发送成就解锁事件和累计分数。

应用为每位玩家在本地记录已计分的公共拼图，防止同一完成记录被重复累计；个人照片拼图不参与该计算。我们不向 Play Games 发送导入照片、照片名称或未完成的拼块布局，也不请求访问邮箱、联系人或 Google Drive。该功能不提供云存档。玩家资料、成就和分数的可见性由 Google 服务设置控制。Play Games 不可用时仍可继续拼图。

5. 同意与控制

正式广告使用 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）检查同意状态，并在需要时展示隐私消息，包括欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士。仅当 UMP 允许时才请求商业广告。需要提供隐私选项入口时，可在设置 → 广告隐私选项中管理选择。测试包在同意服务不可用时可能仍请求 Google 测试广告。

支持的 Android 广告设置允许重置或删除广告 ID，具体菜单名称因设备而异。更改 ID 或拒绝个性化广告，并不必然终止所有广告相关处理。激励广告由你自行选择观看。你可在 Google 设置中管理 Play Games 资料可见性与游戏数据，在设备或备份服务设置中管理系统备份。

6. 照片与权限

选择使用照片制作拼图时，应用打开系统文件或照片选择器，读取你选中的图片，为拼图生成本地副本并保存在设备上。应用不将所选照片上传到我们的图库服务器、广告服务或 Play Games。连接云服务的选择器或 Android 备份可能根据你的设置及相应服务商政策独立处理照片副本。

应用及集成 SDK 使用网络访问、网络状态和广告相关权限或系统服务。游戏不申请访问通讯录、相机、麦克风、设备精确位置或整个照片库。

7. 保存、删除与信息请求

你可以在我的拼图中删除导入照片和拼图记录。清除应用存储或卸载应用会删除本地数据，包括下载图片及本地 Play Games 同步记录。系统备份需单独管理，恢复备份可能还原旧数据。删除本地拼图记录不会撤销已发送给 Google 的成就或分数。

支持邮件仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留。Google、Cloudflare 和 GitHub 根据各自政策及设置保存其独立处理的信息。清除本地数据或重置广告 ID，不会删除这些服务商已经持有的信息。

对我们持有信息的访问、更正、删除或其他请求，请通过下方联系方式提出。对于 Play Games 玩家资料或服务商独立持有的其他信息，请使用相应服务商的隐私及账号管理渠道，我们无法直接删除其独立持有的记录。

8. 儿童与监护人

应用不询问出生日期。监护人可管理设备广告、应用数据、备份及 Google 账号设置。如果你认为儿童直接向我们发送了个人信息，请联系我们，以便调查并采取适当措施。服务商的数据处理方式见上文所链接的政策。

9. 更新与联系方式

政策变更时，我们会在此页面发布并更新日期。如有隐私问题或信息请求，请联系 Jigsaw Moments 的 AiSiFun 开发者：leo344937116@gmail.com。
