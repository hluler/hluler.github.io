Privacy Policy — Little Season

Also known as 拾光小屋 · Developer: AiSiFun
Android package: com.aisifun.littleseason
Updated: 6 November 2026

Little Season is a merge-and-renovate game. You can play its core game offline without an account. This policy describes device storage, optional neighborhood and cloud features, included third-party software, and support communications.

Current 0.8.x internal editions disable ad requests and real-money purchases. They still include advertising and Google Play Billing SDKs. The absence of displayed ads does not mean that the package contains no third-party components. Future activation requires updated configuration and applicable privacy choices.

1. Information kept on your device

The app stores your board, items, progress, virtual resources, orders, room renovations, reward receipts, and sound, vibration and animation preferences in app-private storage. Optional connection settings and session credentials are also stored there. This enables continued play and duplicate-reward protection. Android application backup is disabled. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local progress; a manually uploaded cloud copy, if any, is separate.

2. Optional account and online features

Online features require you to configure an HTTPS neighborhood service and register or sign in. The current internal edition does not automatically connect your game to a server. Our test service is sunnynook-test-api.david-he1986.workers.dev, hosted by Cloudflare. If you choose another operator's service, that operator is responsible for its data practices.

Registration and sign-in send a chosen username and password over HTTPS. Our service stores a salted password-derived hash, not the plain-text password, and hashed session tokens. An account identifier, nickname and registration time support account management. Sessions expire after 30 days. We do not request a phone number or email for registration.

When you manually upload progress, the service stores the game-save file and revision, including items, resources, achievements and receipt history. Friends, gift requests, club membership and in-game gift inbox records support the features you use. Nicknames, club names, member lists and uploaded progress may be visible to other players in the corresponding features. There is no free-text chat, photo upload, voice recording or address-book import. IP-derived rate-limit keys help protect the service against abuse; they expire after the short rate-limit window. Hosting providers also process network information to deliver and protect requests.

3. Advertising, billing and third-party components

The package includes Unity LevelPlay/ironSource, Unity Ads, Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Google Play Billing. Ad requests and real purchases are disabled in the current internal editions. No separate Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or business-analytics SDK is integrated.

When advertising components operate, their documented data categories include device/advertising/app-set identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from IP, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics/performance information. Providers use these for advertising, analytics, service operation, security and fraud prevention, and may share data under their policies. SDK lifecycle components can run with the app; disabling ad requests is not a claim that all local SDK processing is absent.

If advertising is enabled in a later edition, the app provides age and privacy controls before requesting ads. The implemented age gate disables ads for ages under 16; older users still require the applicable consent flow. The app does not request a precise birth date. You can manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Real purchases, if enabled later, would be processed by Google Play; verification would use a purchase token and account-bound receipt, not your payment-card details. Updated disclosures will accompany activation.

Provider information: Unity privacy policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google privacy policy, Cloudflare privacy policy. This public policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes visitor network data under the GitHub privacy statement. The policy page itself contains no advertising or analytics scripts.

4. Permissions, security and age range

The app includes internet/network-state, vibration, billing and advertising-related permissions and SDK components. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, media-library or precise-location access. Release connections require HTTPS. App-private storage and server authentication protect records, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

The game is intended for ages 13 and above, not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us to request review and deletion where applicable.

5. Account and data deletion

In the app: open Settings (设置) → Neighborhood and cloud save (邻里与云存档), sign in to the same service, choose Delete account (删除账号), and enter your password in the app to confirm. Our service deletes the account, password hash, sessions, uploaded save, friendship links, club membership and associated gift inbox records. An owned club may transfer to another member; the shared club record can remain. Local offline progress remains until cleared separately in Android settings.

Without reinstalling the app: email david.he1986@gmail.com with the subject “Little Season account deletion”, the username and the service address used. You may also request deletion of selected data, access or correction. Do not email your password, session token or payment-card information. We may need to verify account ownership before acting; we will request only information necessary for that purpose. For a separately operated server, contact that operator.

Account data is retained while the account exists, unless you delete it or request removal. Successful in-app deletion removes these records from the active service database; we do not retain the deleted game account for marketing. Provider security records may be retained under their own policies. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. The current test service does not process real purchases. Any future legally required transaction-retention rules will be disclosed before purchases are enabled.

6. Support, updates and contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond and resolve the request. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell your account records, game saves or support correspondence. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.

Developer: AiSiFun. Privacy and support: david.he1986@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 拾光小屋

英文名：Little Season · 开发者：AiSiFun
Android 包名：com.aisifun.littleseason
更新日期：2026年11月6日

拾光小屋是一款合成与房屋修复游戏，无需账号即可离线游玩核心玩法。本政策说明本机存储、可选的邻里与云存档、第三方组件和用户支持信息的处理方式。

当前 0.8.x 内部测试版关闭广告请求和真实购买，但仍包含广告及 Google Play Billing SDK。没有展示广告不代表安装包中没有第三方组件。未来启用须完成相应配置与适用的隐私选择。

1. 保存在本机的信息

棋盘、物品、进度、虚拟资源、订单、修复状态、奖励回执，以及声音、振动和动画偏好保存在应用私有存储中。可选连接设置与会话凭据也保存在本机，用于继续游戏和防止重复发奖。应用关闭 Android 应用备份。卸载或清除应用存储会删除本机进度；您主动上传过的云端副本是独立的。

2. 可选账号和联网功能

联网功能需要主动设置 HTTPS 邻里服务地址并注册或登录。当前内部测试版不会自动连接服务器。开发者测试服务位于 sunnynook-test-api.david-he1986.workers.dev，由 Cloudflare 托管。如果您连接其他运营者的服务，该运营者负责其数据处理。

注册及登录通过 HTTPS 传送自选账号和密码；我们的服务保存加盐密码派生值而非明文密码，并保存会话令牌的哈希。账号编号、昵称、注册时间用于账号管理。会话有效期为30天，注册不要求电话或邮箱。

主动上传时，服务保存完整游戏存档和修订号，包括物品、资源、成长与回执历史。好友、赠礼、社团成员关系及礼物信箱记录用于相应功能。昵称、社团名称、成员名单及上传的进度可能在对应界面向其他玩家展示。没有自由文字聊天、照片上传、录音或通讯录导入。服务使用由 IP 派生的短期限流键防止滥用，键在限流窗口结束后过期；托管服务商也会处理提供网络服务和安全保护所需的信息。

3. 广告、支付与第三方组件

安装包包含 Unity LevelPlay/ironSource、Unity Ads、Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen、Google UMP 和 Google Play Billing。当前内部测试版关闭广告请求和真实购买；没有单独接入 Firebase Analytics、Crashlytics 或业务统计 SDK。

广告组件运行时，其官方披露的数据类别包括设备/广告/App Set 标识符、IP 及其推断的大致位置、应用和广告交互、诊断与性能信息，用于广告、分析、服务运行、安全及反作弊，并可能按各自政策分享。SDK 生命周期组件可能随应用运行；关闭广告请求不等于不存在任何 SDK 本机处理。

以后版本启用广告时，会在请求前提供年龄及隐私选项。已实现的年龄门控对16岁以下关闭广告，较大年龄用户仍须经过适用的同意流程，不要求准确生日。广告标识符可在 Android 设置中管理。真实购买如在后续启用，将由 Google Play 处理，并以购买令牌和账号回执验单，不收集您的银行卡信息；启用时会更新对应披露。

第三方说明：Unity 隐私政策、Google 广告数据披露、Google 隐私政策、Cloudflare 隐私政策。本政策托管于 GitHub Pages，访问网络信息由 GitHub 隐私声明规范。本页面没有广告或分析脚本。

4. 权限、安全和年龄

应用包含互联网/网络状态、振动、支付及广告相关权限和 SDK 组件，不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录、媒体库或精确定位访问。Release 连接要求 HTTPS。我们使用应用私有存储和服务端认证保护记录，但任何系统都不能保证绝对安全。

游戏面向13岁及以上玩家，不以13岁以下儿童为目标受众。如认为儿童提供了个人信息，请联系我们核查，并在适用时删除。

5. 删除账号与数据

应用内：设置 → 邻里与云存档 → 登录相同服务 → 删除账号，在应用中输入密码确认。我们的服务会删除账号、密码派生值、会话、已上传存档、好友关系、社团成员关系和关联礼物信箱。您创建的社团可能转交其他成员，共享社团记录可能保留。本机离线进度仍保留，需另行在 Android 设置中清除。

无需重新安装应用：发送邮件至 david.he1986@gmail.com，主题注明“拾光小屋账号删除”，提供账号名和所连接服务地址。也可以申请删除部分数据、访问或更正。不要发送密码、会话令牌或银行卡信息。处理前可能需要核实账号归属，我们仅索取必要的信息。自行连接的其他服务请联系相应运营者。

账号存在期间会保留账号数据，直到删除或申请移除。应用内删除成功后，相应记录会从活动数据库清除，不为营销保留已删除账号。服务商安全记录可能按其政策保留。支持邮件仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保存。当前测试服务不处理真实交易；未来如需要依法保留交易记录，会在启用支付前说明。

6. 支持、更新和联系方式

主动发送邮件时，我们会收到邮箱及您提供的信息，用于回应和处理请求。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。我们不出售账号记录、游戏存档或支持邮件。政策更新会在本页公布并修改日期。
