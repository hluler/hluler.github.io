Privacy Policy — Little Season

Also known as 拾光小屋 · Developer: AiSiFun
Android package: com.aisifun.littleseason
Public offline edition: 0.8.2 (12) · Updated: 6 November 2026

Little Season is an offline merge-and-renovate game. This public edition has no account registration, cloud saves, online social features, advertising, real-money purchases, or third-party analytics. Earlier internal-test editions are described separately below.

1. Local game data

Your board, items, orders, room renovations, virtual resources, progress and game settings are saved in the application's private storage to continue play and remember preferences. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. Android application backup is disabled. The app does not collect or share personal information, device advertising identifiers, location or usage analytics.

2. Permissions and third parties

The offline edition uses the normal vibration permission for feedback. It has no internet, network-state, advertising-ID or billing permission, and contains no LevelPlay/ironSource, Unity Ads, Google Mobile Ads, UMP, Google Play Billing, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics SDK. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, media-library or location access.

Privacy and support links open in your chosen browser or email app. You choose whether to send a message; the game does not attach your saves or identifiers. Google Play and Android may process store, installation or system information under their own policies. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose processing of visitor network data is covered by the GitHub privacy statement. This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts.

3. Support and age range

If you email support, we receive your email address and information you provide, use it to respond, and keep it only as needed for the request and applicable obligations. Do not send passwords, tokens, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. We do not sell game saves or support correspondence.

The game is intended for ages 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. It does not ask for a name, birth date or age profile. A parent or guardian may contact us about information sent to support by a child.

4. Data deletion and your choices

Offline edition: no game account is created. Delete local data by opening Android Settings → Apps → Little Season → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling the app. This removes local progress permanently; the offline edition has no cloud copy to restore. To request access, correction or deletion of information sent to support, email david.he1986@gmail.com. We may request only information necessary to identify the correspondence and verify the request. Device security also depends on Android settings; no system guarantees absolute security.

5. Earlier internal-test accounts

Version 0.8.1-internal (11) had optional, manually configured neighborhood/cloud accounts and bundled advertising SDKs, with ad requests and real purchases disabled. This functionality is absent from the public offline edition. The test service at sunnynook-test-api.david-he1986.workers.dev may still hold accounts that testers voluntarily created: usernames, nicknames, salted password hashes, hashed sessions (30-day expiry), manually uploaded saves, friendships, club memberships and gift inbox records. Cloudflare hosts the service. Account data is retained while the account exists. Public nicknames, club names and uploaded progress were visible in those features.

Delete an earlier account: from the earlier internal app, open 设置 → 邻里与云存档 → sign in to the same service → 删除账号. This removes account credentials, sessions, uploaded saves, friendship and membership links and associated inbox records from the active database; a shared club may transfer to another member. Or, without reinstalling that app, email david.he1986@gmail.com with the username and service address, subject “Little Season account deletion”. Never send your password or session token. We verify ownership before deletion. For an independently operated service, contact that operator. Provider security records may be retained under their own policies. The current test service does not process real purchases.

The earlier package included Unity LevelPlay/ironSource, Unity Ads, Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen and UMP. Their documented operating data includes device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app/ad interactions and diagnostics. Refer to Unity, Google and Cloudflare policies for provider practices. These SDKs are not included in the public offline package.

6. Updates and contact

We will update this page and applicable store disclosures before adding online services, advertising or purchases to a public edition. Developer: AiSiFun. Privacy and support: david.he1986@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 拾光小屋

英文名：Little Season · 开发者：AiSiFun
Android 包名：com.aisifun.littleseason
公开离线版：0.8.2（12）· 更新日期：2026年11月6日

拾光小屋是一款离线合成与房屋修复游戏。本公开版本没有账号注册、云存档、联网社交、广告、真实购买或第三方统计。早期内部测试版本在下文单独说明。

1. 本机游戏数据

棋盘、物品、订单、房屋修复、虚拟资源、进度及游戏设置保存在应用私有存储，用于继续游戏和记住偏好，不传送至开发者服务器。应用关闭 Android 应用备份，不收集或分享个人信息、广告标识符、位置或使用统计。

2. 权限和第三方

离线版仅使用普通振动权限提供反馈，没有互联网、网络状态、广告 ID 或支付权限，不包含 LevelPlay/ironSource、Unity Ads、Google Mobile Ads、UMP、Google Play Billing、Firebase Analytics 或 Crashlytics SDK，不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录、媒体库或定位访问。

隐私和支持链接由您选择的浏览器或邮件应用打开，是否发送邮件由您决定，游戏不会附加存档或标识符。Google Play 和 Android 可按其政策处理商店、安装或系统信息。本政策托管于 GitHub Pages，其访问网络数据处理受 GitHub 隐私声明规范。本页面没有广告或分析脚本。

3. 支持和年龄范围

主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到邮箱及您提供的信息，仅用于回应请求，并在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留。请勿发送密码、令牌、支付资料或不必要的敏感信息。我们不出售游戏存档或支持邮件。

游戏面向13岁及以上玩家，不以13岁以下儿童为目标受众，不索取姓名、生日或年龄档案。家长或监护人可就儿童向支持邮箱发送的信息联系我们。

4. 删除数据和您的选择

离线版：不创建游戏账号。您可通过 Android 设置 → 应用 → 拾光小屋 → 存储 → 清除存储，或卸载应用，永久删除本机数据。离线版没有可恢复的云端副本。对支持邮件中的信息，可联系 david.he1986@gmail.com 申请访问、更正或删除。我们仅索取识别相关邮件和核实请求所必需的信息。设备安全也取决于 Android 设置，任何系统均不能保证绝对安全。

5. 早期内部测试账号

0.8.1-internal（11）曾提供需要手动配置的邻里及云存档账号，包含广告 SDK，但关闭广告请求和真实购买。这些功能不在公开离线版内。测试服务 sunnynook-test-api.david-he1986.workers.dev 可能保留测试者主动创建的账号名、昵称、加盐密码派生值、会话哈希（30天到期）、手动上传的存档、好友关系、社团成员关系和礼物信箱。服务由 Cloudflare 托管，账号存在期间保留相关数据；昵称、社团名称及上传进度曾在相应功能中向其他玩家展示。

删除早期账号：在原内部测试应用的“设置 → 邻里与云存档”登录同一服务后选择“删除账号”，会从活动数据库删除账号凭据、会话、云存档、好友及成员关系和关联信箱；共享社团可能移交其他成员。也可无需重新安装，发送主题为“拾光小屋账号删除”的邮件至 david.he1986@gmail.com，提供账号名和服务地址，不要发送密码或会话令牌。核实归属后处理。独立运营的其他服务器请联系相应运营者。服务商安全记录可按其政策保留。测试服务不处理真实购买。

早期包包含 Unity LevelPlay/ironSource、Unity Ads、Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen 和 UMP，其官方运行数据类别包括设备标识符、IP 推断的大致位置、应用/广告交互及诊断。服务商处理方式见 Unity、Google 和 Cloudflare 政策。这些 SDK 不包含在公开离线包中。

6. 更新与联系

未来公开版本加入联网、广告或购买前，会更新本页及适用的商店披露。开发者：AiSiFun。隐私及客服邮箱：david.he1986@gmail.com。
