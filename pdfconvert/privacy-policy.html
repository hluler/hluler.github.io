Privacy Policy · PDF Converter

Developer: AiSiFun
Android package: com.aisifun.pdfconverter
Last updated: 25 October 2026
Contact: leo344937116@gmail.com

PDF Converter helps you create, scan, read and edit documents. Document conversion and optical character recognition (OCR) take place on your device. The app also integrates Google services for advertising, privacy choices and optional ad-free subscriptions. These services use network connections; “local conversion” does not mean that advertising is offline.

1. Documents, images, camera and OCR

When you select files or folders with Android's system picker, PDF Converter reads the content you authorize and keeps working copies in its private app storage. The camera is used only when you open the scanning feature and grant permission. Images, document contents, signatures, annotations and recognized text are processed on your device. We do not upload these materials to an OCR, conversion or advertising server. Exporting or sharing happens only through an action you choose; the receiving app or service then handles that copy under its own policy.

2. Local preferences and password recovery

The app stores document listings, favorites, language and appearance preferences, unfinished image selections and temporary conversion files locally. If you enable password recovery, saved PDF passwords are encrypted using Android Keystore. The security answer is stored as a salted hash. This does not provide remote recovery, and uninstalling or clearing app data can permanently remove the recovery information. The app does not create a developer account or require you to sign in to us.

3. Google AdMob advertising and privacy choices

Free use may display interstitial ads at natural pauses after completed document work. We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device/account identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not provide your document contents to the ad SDK. We have not added a separate developer analytics SDK.

Where required, a consent message is presented before requesting ads. When required by the consent service, a “Privacy choices” entry in Settings lets you revisit your choices. You can also change advertising privacy controls in Android settings. Consent refusal does not prevent document tools from working. Test builds use Google's demo ad units; a screen marked “LOCAL QA” is a local simulation, not a paid advertisement or an ad impression.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

4. Optional monthly ad-free subscription

Real subscriptions, when available, are purchased and managed through Google Play. Google processes your payment details; we do not receive your card number. The app receives product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status and acknowledgment information to restore and verify ad-free access. When the purchase verification service is enabled, the app sends the purchase token over HTTPS to our verification service, which checks subscription status with the Google Play Developer API. It does not send your documents, images or signatures. Tokens are not included in our application logs. Hosting infrastructure may process request metadata for security and operation.

Verified subscription status and its expiry are cached on the device. Verification is refreshed when the app returns to the foreground; an unverified new or pending purchase does not grant access. Local QA subscription switches do not charge money and are excluded from production behavior. See the subscription terms and Google Play subscription management.

5. Sharing, support and service providers

We do not sell your documents or use them to train models. Google and our hosting providers process the service data described above. If you email support, we receive the message and any attachments you voluntarily send so we can respond. Please avoid sending confidential documents unless necessary. Links and shared files opened in other apps are governed by those services' policies.

6. Retention, deletion and security

You can delete document copies from the app and clear password recovery in Settings. Android Settings → Apps → PDF Converter → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling the app, removes its private local data. Files you exported or shared remain outside the app until you delete them separately. Removing the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Google retains its own service data according to its policies. Contact us for questions or requests concerning information you sent directly to support. We use app-private storage, encrypted password recovery and HTTPS service connections; no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children and changes

PDF Converter is a general document utility and is not designed for children. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can address it. We may update this policy when features or providers change; the current version and date are published here and linked from the app.
