Privacy Policy — Pop Star Crush

App: Pop Star Crush · Developer: AiSiFun / ljstu
Package: com.ljstu.popstar
Updated: 3 November 2026

Pop Star Crush is a candy puzzle game with cumulative scores. You can play offline without creating an app account. From version 1.4.0, the Android app uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and a preinstalled Google AdMob adapter. Networks may be enabled or disabled in the mediation dashboard; AdMob serving is initially disabled in this update. Older versions use AdMob directly. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) continues to manage the existing advertising consent flow. This policy describes local saves, advertising data and your choices.

1. Local game data and support

Your current board, level, cumulative score, best score, helper inventory, daily-gift state, settings and selected age range are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that receives these saves or provide our own cloud saves. Android backup or device migration may copy app data according to your system settings.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond and retain it only as needed for the request and applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive details.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The app offers optional rewarded videos for helpers and occasional interstitials between completed levels. The reward is shown before you choose to watch. No banner is displayed during gameplay. Ad availability does not prevent offline play.

Google and Unity/ironSource advertising SDKs may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, device/account identifiers such as advertising and app-set IDs, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics or performance information. Purposes include advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing depends on age treatment, consent, region and device settings; advertising identifiers and personalisation are restricted for child-treated requests. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. We can see aggregated mediation and network reports. SDK startup components may perform limited setup before mediation initialization; an advertising gate is not a claim that every SDK component performs no processing.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about partner apps for Google's processing, partners, retention and international transfers. This version does not include Play Games sign-in, online leaderboards, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

Unity/ironSource processing, retention, international transfers and privacy requests are described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. This game does not send a developer-defined account ID or purchase receipts to the mediation service and has no in-app purchases. Use the providers' controls for data held by those providers.

3. Age range and children

Pop Star Crush is intended for players aged 6 and above, including children. Before advertising starts, a neutral screen asks for an age range: 12 and under, 13–15, or 16 and over. We do not ask for a name or exact birthday. The range is saved locally and used to configure mediated-network age protections and Google consent requests; we do not send it to a developer-operated server.

For both ranges below 16, we apply Google's child-restricted advertising treatment and mark consent requests as under age of consent. These users are not asked to consent to personalised ads. Google requests use the general-audience content rating limit. Mediated networks receive child-protection signals for both under-16 ranges, with advertising-ID access restricted and the Families SDK filter enabled. No advertising is requested before a range is selected. You can correct the range in Settings → Privacy & ads → Age range. Parents or guardians may contact us about information they believe was provided by a child.

4. Consent and privacy choices

UMP refreshes consent information at launch and shows a message when required for eligible users. Production ads are requested only when UMP permits requests. If permission cannot be established, gameplay remains available without those ads. Where required, Settings → Privacy & ads → Ad privacy choices lets you review or withdraw choices. The same menu links to this policy.

Supported Android settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID. Changing a choice or deleting an ID does not automatically erase previously collected information or stop every form of advertising-related processing. Changing privacy choices discards cached rewarded/interstitial inventory before new ads can be requested under the updated state.

5. Permissions and security

The app uses network access, network-state information and advertising-related permissions. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, media-library or precise-location access. We do not sell local game saves or maintain app-account profiles. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local saves remain until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Check Android backup settings separately if you want to remove backup copies. This does not delete data already controlled by Google, Unity/ironSource or their partners; use their privacy controls and policies for those records. No Pop Star Crush account needs to be deleted.

For information you directly sent to us, email the contact below to request access, correction or deletion, or exercise applicable privacy rights. We may ask only for information needed to identify and respond to the request. Do not send government IDs unless a separate, necessary verification process is explained.

7. Updates and contact

We publish changes on this page with an updated date. Privacy and support contact: leo344937116@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — Pop Star Crush

应用：Pop Star Crush · 开发者：AiSiFun / ljstu
包名：com.ljstu.popstar
更新日期：2026年11月3日

Pop Star Crush 是一款累计得分的糖果消除游戏，无需注册应用账号即可离线游玩。从 1.4.0 版起，Android 版使用 Unity LevelPlay 聚合，预置 ironSource Ads、Unity Ads 和 Google AdMob 适配器。广告源可在聚合后台启停；本次更新初始停用 AdMob 投放。旧版本直接使用 AdMob。Google 用户消息平台（UMP）继续提供现有广告同意流程。本政策说明本地存档、广告相关数据及您的管理方式。

1. 本地数据与用户支持

当前棋盘、关卡、累计分数、最高分、道具数量、每日礼物状态、设置和所选年龄范围保存在本机。我们不运营接收这些存档的服务器，也不提供自建云存档。Android 备份或设备迁移可能根据您的系统设置复制应用数据。

如果您发邮件联系支持，我们会收到您的邮箱及主动提供的信息，用于回应请求，并仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留。请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感信息。

2. 广告与第三方数据

应用提供自愿观看的激励视频以补充道具，并可能在部分已完成关卡之间展示插屏。观看前会说明奖励；游戏操作期间没有横幅广告。广告不可用时仍可离线游戏。

Google 和 Unity/ironSource 广告 SDK 可能收集和共享由 IP 推断的大致位置、设备或账号标识（例如广告 ID 和应用集 ID）、应用或广告互动、诊断和性能信息，用于广告、效果衡量、分析及防止欺诈。具体处理取决于年龄保护、同意状态、地区和设备设置；儿童保护请求会限制广告标识符及个性化处理。SDK 使用 TLS 加密传输。我们可查看聚合平台及广告源的汇总报告。部分 SDK 启动组件可能在聚合初始化前进行有限准备，广告初始化门控并不表示所有 SDK 组件在此前完全没有数据处理。

Google 的处理、合作伙伴、保留期限和跨境传输，请参阅 Google 隐私政策和 Google 如何使用合作应用的信息。本版本没有 Play Games 登录、在线排行榜、Firebase Analytics 或 Crashlytics。

Unity/ironSource 的处理、保留期限、跨境传输及隐私请求，请参阅 Unity 游戏玩家和应用用户隐私政策。本游戏没有内购，不向聚合平台发送开发者自定义账号 ID 或购买凭证。第三方持有的数据请通过相应服务商的隐私工具管理。

3. 年龄范围与儿童

Pop Star Crush 面向 6 岁及以上玩家，包含儿童。启动广告前，中立的年龄选择界面提供“12 岁及以下”“13–15 岁”“16 岁及以上”三个范围，无需姓名或具体生日。范围保存在本机，用于设置聚合广告源的年龄保护及 Google 同意请求；不会传给我们运营的服务器。

两个未满 16 岁的范围均使用 Google 儿童限制广告处理，并将同意请求标记为未达到同意年龄，不向这些玩家索取个性化广告同意。Google 广告请求使用适合一般受众的内容评级限制。两个未满 16 岁范围都会向聚合网络传递儿童保护信号，限制广告 ID 访问并启用 Families SDK 筛选。未选择年龄范围前不请求广告。您可在“设置 → 隐私与广告 → 年龄范围”更正选择。家长或监护人如认为儿童提供了个人信息，可联系我们。

4. 同意与隐私选择

UMP 在启动时更新同意信息，并在要求时向适用用户展示消息。正式版只有在 UMP 允许时才请求广告；无法确定许可时仍可继续游戏。在需要提供入口的情况下，可通过“设置 → 隐私与广告 → 广告隐私偏好”查看或撤回选择。同一菜单提供本政策入口。

您还可以在支持的 Android 设置中重置或删除广告 ID。改变选择或删除 ID 不会自动删除此前收集的数据，也不必然停止所有广告相关处理。修改广告隐私选择时，会先废弃缓存的激励及插屏，再按更新后的状态请求广告。

应用使用网络、网络状态及广告相关权限，不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录、媒体库或精确位置权限。我们不出售本地存档，也不维护应用账号资料库。任何系统均无法保证绝对安全。
