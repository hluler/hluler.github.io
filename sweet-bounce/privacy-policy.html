Sweet Bounce — Privacy Policy

App: Sweet Bounce / 甜心弹弹
Developer: AiSiFun
Android package: com.aisifun.sweetbounce
Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how AiSiFun's Sweet Bounce Android game handles information, including through Google services. You can play the game without an account with AiSiFun. We do not run a separate player-account service or a server that stores your game saves.

1. Information kept on your device

The game saves your unlocked levels, scores, stars, current game, daily challenges, virtual currency, selected balls, items, language and sound preferences in the app's local storage. Local diagnostic records may contain game state and service errors for troubleshooting. These files are not automatically uploaded to an AiSiFun server.

These records support gameplay and let you continue later. Android may include app data in system backups or device transfers, depending on your settings. Such copies are handled by your system or backup provider.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

Sweet Bounce integrates Google AdMob. Interstitials can appear between completed levels; rewarded interstitials offer an in-game bonus. Before a rewarded interstitial, the game explains the reward and lets you cancel.

AdMob may process IP-based approximate location, advertising and app-set identifiers, applicable account identifiers, app/ad interactions, and device or performance diagnostics. These support ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. We can view AdMob performance reports. Google describes SDK traffic as encrypted in transit. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about partner apps.

A Google test advertisement still involves a request to Google's services. A local demonstration explicitly labelled “Test ad” in a development build is a simulation, rather than a Google-delivered advertisement.

3. Advertising consent and your choices

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a privacy message is needed. Where required, it presents choices before production ad requests are allowed. When Google requires a privacy-options entry, use Settings → Privacy choices to review or change your choices. Test builds may request test ads even when a test consent form is unavailable.

Android's advertising controls may let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Options vary by device. These controls, or declining personalised advertising, do not necessarily stop every form of advertising-related processing. You can decline a rewarded advertisement and continue ordinary gameplay.

4. Optional Google Play Games

In versions where Google Play Games is available, Google manages authentication and your gamer profile under your Google settings, including any automatic sign-in preference. An AiSiFun account is not required.

When authenticated, the game can submit your highest completed level, best score and achievement unlocks to Google. A pending-sync record is stored locally so a failed submission can be retried. Google may associate game activity with your gamer identity and process service diagnostics. Other players' access to your profile, scores and achievements depends on Google's service and visibility settings.

The game does not request your Google password, email inbox, contacts, friends list or Google Drive files. Play Games integration does not back up the full game save. Offline play remains available without these online features. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Play Games data-deletion instructions.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle your request. Please avoid including passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the matter and meet applicable obligations.

6. Sharing and service providers

Google processes advertising, consent and available Play Games information as described above. Your device's backup provider may process backup copies. We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information we hold when required by law or necessary to address fraud, security or a legal claim.

Google and other providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their policies explain their handling, retention and international processing. Our policy does not replace the policies of an advertiser or website you choose to visit.

7. Removing data and making requests

To remove local progress, preferences and diagnostic files, clear Sweet Bounce's app storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Manage system backups separately: a later restore may bring back previously saved data.

Local deletion does not withdraw scores or achievements already submitted to Google or erase advertising information held by Google. You can delete this game's Play Games records through Google's game/profile controls. If you want to stop later submissions, also review automatic sign-in and remove local progress as appropriate. Use Google's privacy and account tools for information Google holds independently.

For information you have provided directly to AiSiFun, email us to request access, correction, deletion or help with another privacy request. We will handle requests under applicable requirements. We cannot directly remove records independently controlled by Google or a backup provider.

8. Permissions and security

The game and its SDKs use internet access, network status and advertising-related permissions or system services. The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or precise location. It does not include a personal-photo upload feature.

Local records use Android app storage. Google documents encrypted network transport for its ads and Play Games services. Device access, operating-system protections and your backup settings also affect the security of your information.

9. Children and guardians

The game does not ask for a birth date. Parents and guardians can review device advertising, Google account, game-data and backup controls. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to AiSiFun, contact us so we can review and address it. The linked provider policies explain their own processing.

10. This webpage

This policy page does not embed advertising, analytics scripts or remote fonts. When it is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub receives normal web-request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver the page and operate its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link contacts that site's provider.

11. Changes and contact

We will revise the date above when this policy changes. Contact the AiSiFun developer responsible for Sweet Bounce at leo344937116@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

甜心弹弹 — 隐私政策

应用：Sweet Bounce / 甜心弹弹
开发者：AiSiFun
Android 包名：com.aisifun.sweetbounce
更新日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日

本政策介绍 AiSiFun 的甜心弹弹 Android 游戏及其使用的 Google 服务如何处理信息。您无需注册 AiSiFun 账号即可游玩。我们没有独立的玩家账号服务，也不运营保存游戏存档的服务器。

1. 保存在设备上的信息

游戏在应用本地存储中记录已解锁关卡、分数、星数、当前对局、每日挑战、虚拟货币、所选糖果球、道具、语言及声音设置。本地诊断文件可能包含对局状态和服务错误，以便排查问题；这些文件不会自动上传到 AiSiFun 服务器。

这些记录用于运行游戏和继续进度。Android 可能根据您的设置，将应用数据纳入系统备份或设备迁移；相关副本由系统或备份服务商处理。

2. 广告及 Google 处理的信息

甜心弹弹集成 Google AdMob，可在通关后的关卡间隙展示插屏广告，并通过激励插屏提供游戏内奖励。激励插屏开始前会说明奖励，您可以取消。

AdMob 可能处理由 IP 推断的大致位置、广告及应用集标识、适用的账号标识、应用或广告互动、设备及性能诊断，用于广告展示、衡量、分析和防范欺诈。我们可查看 AdMob 效果报表。Google 说明其 SDK 网络传输使用加密。详情见 Google 隐私政策及合作应用信息说明。

Google 测试广告仍会向 Google 服务发送请求。开发测试版本中明确标注“测试广告”的本地演示是模拟流程，不是由 Google 网络提供的广告。

3. 广告同意与选择

Google 用户消息平台（UMP）检查是否需要展示隐私消息，并在适用情况下提供选择，正式广告请求须经其允许。Google 要求提供隐私选项入口时，您可通过设置 → 隐私选择查看或调整选择。测试版本可能在测试同意表单不可用时继续请求测试广告。

Android 的广告设置可能允许重置或删除广告 ID，具体入口因设备而异。这些操作或拒绝个性化广告，不一定停止全部广告相关处理。您可以放弃激励广告并继续普通游戏。

4. 可选的 Google Play Games

在支持 Google Play Games 的版本中，Google 根据您的 Google 设置管理身份验证和玩家资料，包括自动登录偏好；无需注册 AiSiFun 账号。

认证后，游戏可向 Google 提交最高已完成关卡、最高分及成就解锁，并在本地记录待同步状态以重试失败的提交。Google 可能将游戏活动与玩家身份关联，并处理服务诊断。其他玩家能否查看资料、分数和成就，取决于 Google 的服务和可见性设置。

游戏不请求 Google 密码、邮箱内容、通讯录、好友列表或 Google Drive 文件。此集成不备份完整对局存档；不使用在线功能仍可离线游玩。详情见 Google 隐私政策及 Play Games 数据删除说明。

5. 支持邮件

您通过邮件联系我们时，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容及主动提供的附件，用于回复、排查问题和处理请求。请勿发送密码或非必要的敏感信息。支持邮件在解决事项及履行适用义务所需的期间保留。

6. 共享及服务提供方

Google 按上述方式处理广告、同意及可用的 Play Games 信息；设备的备份提供方可能处理备份副本。我们不以出售个人信息换取金钱。对于我们持有的信息，可能因法律要求，或处理欺诈、安全及法律争议的需要而披露。

Google 及其他服务提供方可能在您所在国家以外处理信息，其政策说明相关处理、保留及国际传输安排。您主动访问广告或外部网站时，该网站的政策同样需要留意，本政策不替代第三方政策。

7. 删除数据及提出请求

您可在 Android 设置中清除甜心弹弹的应用存储，或卸载应用，以移除本地进度、设置及诊断文件。系统备份需单独管理，后续恢复备份可能重新带回原有数据。

删除本地数据不会撤回已经提交至 Google 的分数、成就，也不会删除 Google 持有的广告信息。您可在 Google 的游戏或玩家资料管理中删除本游戏的 Play Games 数据；如需停止后续提交，也请检查自动登录设置，并按需移除本地进度。Google 独立持有的信息应通过其隐私及账号工具管理。

对于您直接提供给 AiSiFun 的信息，可通过邮件提出访问、更正、删除或其他隐私请求，我们会按适用要求处理。我们无法直接删除由 Google 或备份服务商独立控制的记录。

8. 权限与安全

游戏及其 SDK 使用互联网、网络状态和广告相关权限或系统服务。游戏不请求相机、麦克风、通讯录、照片库或精确位置权限，也没有上传个人照片的功能。

本地记录保存在 Android 应用存储中。Google 说明其广告及 Play Games 服务采用加密网络传输。设备访问权限、系统保护措施及备份设置也会影响信息安全。

9. 儿童与监护人

游戏不询问出生日期。监护人可检查设备广告、Google 账号、游戏数据和备份设置。如您认为儿童直接向 AiSiFun 提供了个人信息，请联系我们，以便核查并处理。服务提供方的独立处理方式见所链接的政策。

10. 本网页

本政策网页没有嵌入广告、分析脚本或远程字体。通过 GitHub Pages 提供本页面时，GitHub 会收到 IP 地址、浏览器信息等普通网页请求信息，以传送页面并运行服务，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。点击外部链接会联系对应网站提供方。

11. 更新与联系方式

政策变更时，我们会更新本页日期。关于甜心弹弹的隐私问题或信息请求，请联系负责本应用的 AiSiFun 开发者：leo344937116@gmail.com。
