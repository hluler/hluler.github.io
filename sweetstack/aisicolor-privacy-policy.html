Privacy Policy — AisiColor

App: AisiColor: Paint by Number (绘色时光)
Developer: AiSiFun
Package: com.aisifun.happycolor
Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how AisiColor and its integrated services handle information. You can color pictures without registering an account. We do not upload your coloring saves to our artwork server.

1. Coloring data and support

Picture progress, fill order, palettes, favorites, hint counts, preferences and downloaded artwork are stored on your device. They support continuing pictures, replaying your coloring process and offline play. Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device and system settings. We do not provide our own cloud-save service.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Artwork downloads and website hosting

The app downloads public catalogs, previews and artwork files over HTTPS from art.liteps.cc, hosted through Cloudflare. Serving these requests involves your IP address, requested resource, time and technical request information. The hosting service may process this information for content delivery, caching, security and diagnostics. We do not attach your saved progress, fill history, favorites or Google player ID to these downloads.

This privacy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web requests to GitHub. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement for these providers' practices.

3. Advertising and Google services

AisiColor uses Google AdMob for occasional interstitial ads after newly completed pictures and optional rewarded interstitial ads for three hints. Viewing a rewarded ad is your choice; closing before the reward condition is met does not earn the ad reward.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information including:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • advertising, device and, where applicable, account identifiers, including Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, such as launches, video views and taps;
  • device and diagnostic information, including app and SDK performance.

Google and advertising partners process these data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. We may view aggregate advertising performance reports in AdMob. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services, including international processing.

If Google Play Games is enabled in an app version and you connect it, Google may process your player profile, authentication information, achievements and completed-picture leaderboard score under its policies. This integration does not send your coloring saves or fill history to Google, request your email or Google Drive access, or send your Google player ID to our artwork server. You can continue coloring when Games is unavailable.

4. Consent and your choices

Production versions use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request updated consent information and show a privacy message when required, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. Advertising is requested only when UMP reports that requests are allowed. When a privacy-options entry is required, find More → Ad privacy choices in the app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID through supported Android advertising controls; menu names vary. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily stop every form of advertising-related processing. Clearly identified offline ad demonstrations in test builds are local simulations, not commercial impressions.

5. Permissions and sharing

The app and SDKs use network access, network-state information and advertising-related Android permissions or services. We do not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise-location access.

Sharing a completed picture opens the Android share chooser only when you select Share. Your chosen receiving app handles the image according to its own policy. AisiColor does not automatically send the image to other people or upload it to our artwork server.

6. Retention, deletion and requests

Local data remain until removed through app functions, Android's clear-storage controls or uninstallation. Manage system backups separately: restoring a backup may restore previous data. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle requests and applicable obligations. Hosting and advertising providers retain their information according to their own policies and service settings; we do not set a single retention period for their independently held data.

Clearing local storage does not delete data already held independently by Google or hosting providers, and resetting an advertising ID does not itself erase prior data. Contact us for access, correction, deletion or other requests concerning information we hold. Requests for independently held provider data should use the providers' channels.

7. Children and guardians

The app does not ask for a date of birth. Parents or guardians can manage device advertising and app-data controls. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Third-party processing is described in the policies linked above.

8. Changes and contact

We publish policy changes here and update the date. For privacy questions or requests, contact the AiSiFun developer of AisiColor at leo344937116@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 绘色时光

应用：AisiColor: Paint by Number（绘色时光）
开发者：AiSiFun
包名：com.aisifun.happycolor
更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

本政策说明 AisiColor 及其集成服务如何处理信息。填色无需注册账号，我们不向自有画库服务器上传你的填色存档。

1. 填色数据与用户支持

画作进度、填色顺序、配色、收藏、提示计数、偏好和下载画作保存在设备本地，用于继续填色、回放创作过程和离线游玩。Android 可能根据设备与系统设置备份或迁移应用数据；我们不提供自建云存档服务。

你通过邮件联系我们时，我们收到你的邮箱地址及你主动提供的内容，用于处理请求。请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感信息。

2. 画库下载与网页托管

应用通过 HTTPS 从由 Cloudflare 托管的 art.liteps.cc 下载公开目录、缩略图与画作文件。响应下载涉及 IP 地址、资源路径、时间及请求技术信息；托管服务可能将其用于内容分发、缓存、安全和诊断。下载请求不附带你的存档、填色历史、收藏或 Google 玩家 ID。

本隐私网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问会向 GitHub 发送普通网页请求。托管服务的处理方式见 Cloudflare 隐私政策和 GitHub 隐私声明。

3. 广告与 Google 服务

AisiColor 使用 Google AdMob，在部分新画作完成后展示普通插屏，并提供可选择观看的奖励插屏，以获得 3 次提示。提前关闭且未达到奖励条件时，不获得广告奖励。

Google 移动广告 SDK 收集并分享的信息包括：

  • IP 地址及由其推断的大致位置；
  • 广告、设备及适用时的账号标识符，例如 Android 广告 ID 和应用集 ID；
  • 应用和广告互动，例如启动、视频观看和点击；
  • 设备与诊断信息，例如应用和 SDK 的性能。

Google 及广告合作伙伴使用这些数据提供广告、分析和防范欺诈。SDK 数据使用 TLS 加密传输，我们可在 AdMob 查看广告效果汇总报表。其处理方式（包括跨境处理）见 Google 隐私政策和 Google 如何使用来自合作应用的信息。

若某版本启用 Google Play Games 且你连接该服务，Google 可能按其政策处理玩家资料、认证信息、成就和完成画作数量排行。该集成不向 Google 发送填色存档或填色顺序，不请求邮箱或 Google Drive 权限，也不向自有画库发送 Google 玩家 ID。Games 不可用时仍可继续填色。

4. 同意与选择

正式版本使用 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）更新同意信息，并按需要展示隐私消息，包括欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士地区。仅当 UMP 返回允许请求广告时，才请求广告。当需要隐私选项入口时，可在更多 → 广告隐私选择管理选择。

支持的 Android 设备提供重置或删除广告 ID 的设置，菜单因设备而异。拒绝个性化广告并不必然停止所有广告相关处理。测试包中明确标识的离线广告演示为本地模拟，不构成商业广告展示。

5. 权限与作品分享

应用及 SDK 使用网络访问、网络状态和广告相关 Android 权限或系统服务。我们不申请通讯录、相机、麦克风或精确位置权限。

只有你主动选择“分享”时才打开 Android 分享选择器；接收应用按其自己的政策处理图片。AisiColor 不自动将作品发送给其他人或上传到自有画库。

6. 保存、删除与信息请求

本地数据保存至通过应用功能、Android 清除存储或卸载删除。系统备份需单独管理，恢复备份可能还原旧数据。支持邮件仅在处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保留。托管和广告服务商按自己的政策和服务设置保留信息，我们不对这些独立持有的数据设定统一期限。

删除本地数据不会删除 Google 或托管服务商已经独立持有的信息；重置广告 ID 本身也不会删除旧记录。对我们持有信息的访问、更正、删除等请求，请通过下方邮箱联系；服务商独立持有的数据请使用其相应渠道。

7. 儿童与监护人

应用不询问出生日期。监护人可管理设备广告与应用数据设置。如果你认为儿童直接向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，以便调查并采取适当措施。第三方处理方式见上文链接的政策。

8. 更新与联系方式

政策变更时在此发布并更新日期。如有隐私问题或信息请求，请联系 AisiColor 的 AiSiFun 开发者：leo344937116@gmail.com。
