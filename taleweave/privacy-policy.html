Privacy Policy — TaleWeave Android

App: TaleWeave (织遇)
Developer: AiSiFun
Package: com.aisifun.taleweave
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers the TaleWeave Android app (com.aisifun.taleweave) and its story service. The service is operated by AiSiFun. For privacy questions or requests, contact david.he1986@gmail.com.

1. Account, story data and support

  • Account and profile: your chosen username, a password hash, profile details and preferences. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your Google account identifier, email address and basic profile information. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Stories: the messages, character definitions, story state, branches, notes, uploads, saved works and comments that you submit. These records allow you to continue a story and use your account across devices.
  • Entitlements: membership status, point balances, usage and reward records. If you use Google Play purchases, purchase identifiers and tokens are sent to the backend for verification; card details are handled by Google Play.
  • Service and device information: connection information, such as IP address, and operational logs used to provide the service, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Please send only what is needed for your request; do not send passwords, API keys or unrelated private conversations.

2. AI services and cloud hosting

To generate replies, relevant conversation context, character information and story memory are sent to the AI provider selected for that story. DeepSeek is currently available; the available models may change in the app. You should avoid entering information you do not want an AI provider to process. Cloudflare hosts the account and story backend; the Aliyun entry point serves application files and forwards API requests to that backend. Processing may take place outside your country.

Our hosting providers process ordinary connection information to deliver content, maintain security and diagnose issues. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The Android app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your region, choices and device settings, Google may process advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent messages are shown where required. Available privacy choices can be opened from the wallet's “Ad privacy choices” entry.

Banner and interstitial ads are used for free accounts; members are exempt from these formats. Rewarded ads are optional and start only when you request one. Reward verification sends a pseudonymous account hash and a random reward-session identifier to Google; we do not put your chat messages, email address or profile text into these reward parameters. Google sends a signed callback to verify eligible rewards. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google ad controls.

The web version does not load the native AdMob SDK. Debug builds use Google test ad units; these requests still contact Google. Clearly labelled local QA demonstrations do not create advertising revenue or issue production points. AdMob reporting may provide us with aggregated advertising-performance information.

4. Google sign-in and purchases

Google sign-in is optional. It requests basic identity, email and profile information so we can create or link your TaleWeave account and restore its stories. It does not request your contacts, Google Drive files or Gmail messages. You can manage access in your Google account settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete the TaleWeave account or its existing stories.

If Google Play purchases are available, Google handles payment processing and sends purchase information for server verification, entitlement delivery and restoration. We do not collect your card number. Test purchases and support grants are distinguished from actual payments in our records.

5. Consent, device controls and permissions

Production ads use UMP to refresh consent information and show a message when required, including in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Commercial ads are requested only when UMP allows them. Open My → Membership & Star Points → Ad privacy choices to access available privacy options. Rewarded ads remain optional.

You can manage or reset advertising identifiers through supported Android settings. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily stop every advertising-related process. The app and its SDKs use network, network-state, billing and advertising-related permissions. They do not request your contacts, camera, microphone, precise device location or broad access to your photo library. Uploads that you choose to submit are sent to the story service; do not upload sensitive documents or images you do not want stored there.

6. Retention, deletion and privacy requests

You can change your profile and language preferences, choose what to publish, and control advertising choices available through UMP and your device. Signing in with Google is optional. Account and story data are retained to operate your account and preserve your stories; operational and transaction records may also be retained for security, fraud prevention and required record keeping. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated data. We will review your request and explain any records that must be retained. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your cloud account.

Signing out, clearing local storage or uninstalling removes local access and preferences as applicable; it does not remove cloud stories, previously published comments or information independently held by Google and other providers. For information a provider controls independently, use that provider's account and privacy channels.

7. Security

We use HTTPS and account authentication to protect access to the service, and store password hashes rather than plaintext passwords. Conversation context is processed by the selected AI service as described above. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and avoid including sensitive personal information in stories.

8. Children and guardians

The service is not intended for children under 13 or the higher minimum age required in their region. Parents and guardians can manage device, advertising and Google account settings. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

9. Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Contact: david.he1986@gmail.com.

隐私政策 — 织遇移动版

应用：TaleWeave（织遇）
开发者：AiSiFun
包名：com.aisifun.taleweave
更新日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

本政策适用于织遇 TaleWeave Android 应用（com.aisifun.taleweave）及其故事服务。服务由 AiSiFun 运营。隐私问题与数据请求请联系：david.he1986@gmail.com。

1. 账户、故事数据与用户支持

  • 账户与资料：用户名、密码哈希、您填写的个人资料和偏好。选择 Google 登录时，我们获取 Google 账户标识、邮箱与基本资料，不会获取您的 Google 密码。
  • 故事内容：您提交的消息、角色设定、剧情状态、分支、笔记、上传内容、收藏及评论，用于保存剧情、继续会话及跨设备恢复。
  • 权益与记录：会员状态、星点余额、使用量和奖励记录。使用 Google Play 购买时，订单标识与购买凭证会发送至后台核验，银行卡资料由 Google Play 处理。
  • 服务及设备信息：提供服务、诊断故障及防止滥用所需的 IP 地址等连接信息与运行日志。

通过邮箱联系支持时，我们会收到您的邮箱地址及您主动提供的说明或附件。请仅提供处理问题所需的信息，不要发送密码、API 密钥或无关的私人对话。

2. AI 服务与云端托管

生成回复时，我们将相关对话上下文、角色信息及故事记忆发送给当前故事所选的 AI 服务提供方。目前可使用 DeepSeek，具体可用模型以应用显示为准。请勿输入您不希望 AI 提供方处理的信息。账户及故事后台托管于 Cloudflare；阿里云入口提供应用文件并转发 API 请求。数据处理可能发生在您所在国家或地区之外。

托管服务商为分发内容、维护安全和诊断服务问题处理普通连接信息。详情见 Cloudflare 隐私政策及 阿里云隐私政策。

3. 广告

Android 应用集成 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）和用户消息平台 UMP。根据地区、您的选择和设备设置，Google 可能处理广告或设备标识、IP 地址及大致位置、广告互动与诊断信息，用于广告投放、衡量效果及防止欺诈。在需要时会显示同意消息；您可通过钱包内的“广告隐私选择”入口管理可用选项。

免费账户可能看到横幅和插屏，会员免除这两类广告。激励广告仅在您主动选择后播放。奖励核验向 Google 发送去标识化的账户哈希和随机奖励会话标识，不在奖励参数中发送聊天正文、邮箱或个人资料文字。Google 通过签名回调核验符合条件的奖励。详情见 Google 隐私政策和 Google 广告控制。

网页版本不加载原生 AdMob SDK。调试版本使用 Google 测试广告位，但测试广告请求仍会连接 Google。明确标注的本地 QA 演示不会产生广告收入，也不会发放正式星点。我们可能通过 AdMob 报告查看汇总的广告效果信息。

4. Google 登录与购买

Google 登录为可选方式，仅请求基本身份、邮箱与资料，用于创建或绑定织遇账户、恢复其故事；不请求通讯录、Google Drive 文件或 Gmail 邮件。您可在 Google 账户设置中管理授权。撤销 Google 授权本身不会删除织遇账户或已有故事。

如 Google Play 购买功能可用，付款由 Google 处理，相关订单信息用于服务端验单、发放权益与恢复购买。我们不会获取您的银行卡号。测试购买及客服补赠会与真实付款分别记录。

5. 同意、设备控制与权限

正式广告通过 UMP 更新同意信息，并在需要时显示隐私消息，包括欧洲经济区、英国及瑞士。仅在 UMP 允许时请求商业广告。可通过我的 → 会员与星点 → 广告隐私选择打开可用选项；激励广告始终由您主动选择。

您可在支持的 Android 设置中管理或重置广告标识。拒绝个性化广告不一定终止全部广告相关处理。应用及 SDK 使用网络、网络状态、计费及广告相关权限，不请求通讯录、相机、麦克风、精确设备位置或照片库的广泛读取权限。您主动提交的上传内容会发送至故事服务，请勿上传不希望保存的敏感文件或图片。

6. 保存期限、删除与隐私请求

您可修改资料和语言偏好、选择公开哪些内容，并通过 UMP 及设备设置管理可用广告选项。Google 登录为可选方式。我们为维护账户、保留剧情而保存账户及故事数据；运行与交易记录还可能因安全、防欺诈或必要记账要求保留。您可联系上述邮箱申请访问、更正或删除账户及关联数据，我们会核对请求并说明必须保留的记录。仅卸载应用不会删除云端账户。

退出登录、清理本地存储或卸载应用，会按相应操作移除本地访问状态或偏好，但不会删除云端故事、已经公开的评论，也不会删除 Google 等提供方独立持有的信息。对于由提供方独立控制的信息，请使用其账户和隐私渠道处理。

7. 安全

我们使用 HTTPS 和账户鉴权保护服务访问，保存密码哈希而非明文密码。对话上下文会按上文说明交由所选 AI 服务处理。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全，请妥善保管账户凭据，并避免在故事中提供敏感个人信息。

8. 儿童与监护人

本服务不面向未满 13 岁，或未达到其地区更高最低年龄要求的儿童。家长及监护人可管理设备、广告及 Google 账户设置。如您认为儿童已向我们提交个人信息，请联系我们，以便核查并采取适当措施。

9. 政策更新与联系

更新将在本页发布并注明新的生效日期。联系邮箱：david.he1986@gmail.com。
